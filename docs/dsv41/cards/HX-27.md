# HX-27: More work for the MI50s: they are not idle, and most candidates do not pay
Status: desk (one zero-build test proposed) · Date: 2026-11-13
Code: existing `DS4_DRAFT_EXPERTS=helper` (in build 15, never timed). Flags: as named.

## What and why
- The premise: the four MI50s look idle most of the time, so give them more of the work.
- This desk study checked the premise against the run logs and ranked eight candidates by expected gain per unit of effort. Nothing was run.

## Prediction
- The only candidate testable without a build, the drafter's routed experts on the MI50s (`DS4_DRAFT_EXPERTS=helper`): one user central **+0.2 ms a token (+2 %, slower)**, range −0.15 to +0.9 ms; four users −0.5 to −1 %. Pre-registered: yes, in the study note, with pass, fail and abort lines.

## Correctness
- Not run.

## Results
| finding | value | label |
|---|---|---|
| are the MI50s idle at decode? | no: they hold every expert that does not fit on the XTX and run a device-direct window in every trunk layer of every token; the lead waits for the slowest helper with a pick | REPORTED (HX-17, HX-18, ANATOMY.md) |
| MI50 busy share | ~4 % of a one-row token; ~10–15 % with 2–4-row verify windows; 78–82 % of windows empty | INFERRED / REPORTED |
| what is really free | ~11 GiB of HBM a card, and compute in 25–75 µs gaps that cannot be filled without delaying the next window | INFERRED |
| past MI50 decode attempts | per-group kernels, DPP, fused down, multi-row kernels, the rewritten kernels and higher power caps: none paid (HX-23, HX-24, HX-25, CP-05); the prompt-side work did (PF-12) | MEASURED (those cards) |
| ranked candidates | drafter experts on the MI50s (+2 %, test); MI50 #5 as a fifth helper after a proper riser (−1 to −4 % time to first token, blocked on hardware); duplicate hot experts across MI50s (−0.5 to −2 % a verify step, 2–4 days); Engram, indexer, prefix KV, a dedicated drafter card, prefill disaggregation: no | INFERRED |

## Verdict
- **The premise does not hold for decode:** the MI50s are on every token's critical path in short bursts. Moving more work into their gaps risks pushing the next window late, which lands directly on every token.
- One zero-build A/B (`DS4_DRAFT_EXPERTS=helper` against the served build, with a placebo) would close the "drafter on the MI50s" question with data; it is expected to show no gain. The fifth MI50 waits for a link better than its current riser (HW-10).
