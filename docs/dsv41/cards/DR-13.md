# DR-13: Tree drafting, measured: a real ceiling of +0.48 tokens a round, eaten by the verify cost of the extra rows
Status: shelved (tree drafting parked until a verify row costs under ~1.5 ms) · Date: 2026-11-19
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (an offline ranking of the drafter's own logits; no engine change). Measurement: greedy speculative evaluation with the drafter's distributions dumped, plus a verify-cost sweep.

## What and why
- DR-09 closed tree speculation on a counting build. This run measures the ceiling directly: when a draft token misses, how often is the right token the drafter's second or third choice, and what would branching on those cost to verify?

## Prediction
- Ceiling bar: at least 0.30 accepted tokens a round from top-3 branches. GO for building trees only if the best tree is at most × 0.97 ms a token.

## Correctness
- Both arms VALID: the offline replay agrees with the engine 100 % (alignment 1.000, 0 offline disagreements) (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 19 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX + 4 × MI50 helpers; native MXFP4 pack, internal native build, expert cache off, greedy; block 3 (545 rounds) and block 6 (530 rounds). Verify cost: one step at 1 to 16 rows.

## Verdict
- **The ceiling is real (+0.48 tokens a round, above the 0.30 bar), but each extra verify row costs 4.69 ms on a 20.2 ms step.** The best tree is 10 % slower; more branches are 28–62 % slower.
- Tree drafting pays only if a verify row costs under about 1.5 ms (INFERRED). The row cost, not the tree, is the lever; the cost curve above is the useful by-product for verify and batching work.
