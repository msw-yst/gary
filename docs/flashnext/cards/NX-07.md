# NX-07: Calibrated Q4_K fits on the GPUs at the same speed
Status: kept (the standard file from cycle 6 on) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: no engine change. File: our calibrated Q4_K ([QUANT.md](../QUANT.md)), packed with `tools/iq_pack.py --compat-bf16`.

## What and why
- The shipped UD-IQ4_XS is ~3.84 bits per weight with mostly 3-bit expert gate/up. Our calibrated Q4_K scores closer to Q8_0 (mean KL 0.032 vs 0.056) but is 106 GB against ~85, with 2.97 MB experts. With the MI50s about half empty, it might fit entirely on GPUs.

## Prediction
- Expected to fit with every expert on a GPU; speed cost unknown.
- Pre-registered: the fit question only.

## Correctness
- Reference solo text sha prefix `a7ae6623797f17fe` for this file; batched == solo on every run (MEASURED, fair round).

## Timing
- Hardware: 2 × RX 7900 XTX 24 GB, 4 × Instinct MI50 16 GB (135 W cap), EPYC 7642 (48 cores), 256 GB DDR4-2666.
- Software: Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes on ROCm 7.2 (the runtime in Strata's ROCm image). Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Workload: 8 prompts × 384 tokens, temperature 0, presence 0; tp2 + 4 MI50, cycle-6 transport; alternating Q U Q U.
