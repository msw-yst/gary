# HW-17: Multi-second freezes: the GPU driver evicting every queue because the registered RAM store's pages move
Status: explained (fix in HW-18) · Date: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22
Code: the exposure comes from `DS4_STORE_DIRECT=1` (PF-20; in release candidate r2a; not in the published release). Runs used internal measurement branches and r2a. Flags: `DS4_STORE_DIRECT=0|1`, `DS4_STORE_GB` (store size).

## What and why
- From 19 Nov some runs showed rare decode tokens of 7–20 s, slow first prompts, and servers taking 8–10 minutes to load. Two decode experiments were first misread: a "stall in one kernel" on the lead XTX was one frozen window (a 1.57 s kernel) inside an otherwise normal run. What freezes the whole process?

## Prediction
- After the first diagnosis: the GPU driver (KFD) evicts the process's queues, triggered through the 217 GB RAM store registered with the GPUs (medium confidence). Pre-registered for the deciding run: yes (arena verdict: unregistered ≤ 5 % of registered eviction time).

## Correctness
- Deciding run: token ids equal across all arms (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 21–22 Nov, the test machine (four XTX + five MI50, all nine GPUs in one process), native MXFP4 pack, 2K prompt, 4,000 decode tokens, with side loggers (KFD `evicted_ms` per device, memory and compaction counters, a 1 ms heartbeat).

## Verdict
- **The registered (userptr) RAM store is the eviction mechanism, and memory compaction is the trigger.** The store's 217 GB are ordinary 4 KB pages that the kernel may move. When compaction moves one, the driver stops every queue of the process on all nine GPUs until each 1.2 GB chunk is re-mapped on every card; repeated moves chain this into seconds (INFERRED from kernel source, matched by the counters).
- Unregistering the store makes evictions all but vanish and loads a server in ~2.2 min instead of 8.5–9.5, at ~+1.4–1.9 s on the first request's prompt read.
- Freeing RAM is not a fix: a 150 GB store with 72 GB free evicted just as much.
- Fix: keep the store registered but unmovable (HW-18).

## Reproduce
- Serve with `DS4_STORE_DIRECT=1` and `=0` and read each GPU's KFD `evicted_ms` for the process over its life.

## Notes / caveats
- No decode freeze happened inside the deciding run (0 tokens > 400 ms in every arm); the evidence is eviction time at load and on first requests, which every registered process shows. The decode freezes are inferred to share the mechanism.
- The same mechanism was met on 7 Nov with a different host buffer and fixed then; registering the whole store reopened it.
- Earlier "observer effect" and "back-to-back start" hypotheses were dropped as chance.

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output) or INFERRED (our own reading).
