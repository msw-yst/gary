# QA-17: Agent tasks (Terminal-Bench 2.1, 27 tasks), the 2.25-bit pack against the hosted model: most of the gap was speed and a 600 s client timeout
Status: finding (the quality part was re-opened by QA-18) · Date: 2026-11-16
Code: none (agent harness settings and the served builds only). Flags: none in the engine; the harness's per-call client timeout and retries.

## What and why
- Agent tasks scored lower on our 2.25-bit pack than on a hosted DeepSeek V4.1 Flash API. We asked whether the loss is quality (the quantisation) or something else: speed, concurrency, harness settings, luck.
- Benchmark: Terminal-Bench 2.1, 27 tasks, one agent per task, the same harness and time limits on both sides.

## Prediction
- Working hypothesis at the start: the 2.25-bit pack loses in-time quality against hosted. After the first reruns the reading changed to "speed and luck, not quantisation". Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- Per task, the first prompt sent to each side had the same length to within 3 tokens, which rules out a different chat template or effort setting; sampling temperature 0.6 on both sides (REPORTED).
- Not a teacher-forced comparison; quality at the token level is QA-11.

## Timing
- 15 Nov: 27 tasks × 4 concurrent agents (N = 4) on each side, then reruns: hosted 3× on the two "quality" tasks, local one agent at a time (N = 1) on the 9 local failures. Local runs used the served build of that day (a build-13 variant) with the 2.25-bit pack on the test machine.
- 16 Nov ("step 2"): the harness's client timeout fixed on both sides (per-call timeout raised to 7,500 s, client retries off), then reruns of the three gap tasks: feal-linear, regex-chess, query-optimize. Local on build 17, one agent at a time.

## Verdict
- **Most of the 27-task gap was speed, not quality.** Hosted decodes 2–5× faster a request, and the harness's client library killed any call longer than 600 s (with retries), which only ever hit the slower local server: at N = 4, regex-chess lost about 78 of its 120 minutes and write-compressor about 23 of 30 to killed calls (REPORTED). Hosted's two "quality" passes on the morning run did not repeat (model-extraction 1/4, query-optimize 2/4).
- **Not all of it.** With the timeout fixed, two tasks still pointed at quality: on feal-linear the 2.25-bit pack never found the short solution hosted found in 3 of 4 runs (Fisher one-sided p ≈ 0.07, n = 4 each, not conclusive), and on query-optimize it stopped early with a wrong answer every time, far inside the time limit. regex-chess was speed and search mixed: local can solve it with 0.36× hosted's tokens, but its other run had not finished at 479K tokens.
- The native-weights rerun of the same tasks settled the quality question: QA-18.

## Reproduce
- Terminal-Bench 2.1 against any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Raise the client's per-call timeout well above your slowest call (we used 7,500 s) and switch client retries off, or slow servers are penalised for being slow per call rather than per task.

## Notes / caveats
- One to four runs a task; pass rates on single tasks are noisy, and feal-linear is bimodal for every model (a short solve or a long run that does not converge).
- The thinking budget: our server caps reasoning at 32,768 tokens only because our config named no budget (a per-request override exists). It was hit 6 times at N = 4 and once at N = 1; hosted truncates at the same 32,768 (16 of 636 calls) (REPORTED).
- At N = 1 the local server spent about 77 % of wall time generating, about 84 tok/s a request all in (REPORTED).
- The task names are Terminal-Bench's own. Prompts, trajectories and the hosted provider's outputs are not published.
