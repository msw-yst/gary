# NX-04: Part of the missed experts over PCIe during the CPU wait (pcie-frac 0.2)
Status: dropped (not exact) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: an existing engine setting; not used in any recommended environment. Flags: `STRATA_SET="pcie-frac=0.2"`.

## What and why
- Under tp2 with a CPU expert path, card 0 waited ~15-20 ms a window for the host's CPU experts. Letting card 0 DMA part of its missed experts from host memory and compute them itself shortens the CPU pass.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- **Not exact.** Solo text changed against base 0/8; batched text against its own solo 0/4 at 4 users (MEASURED). Which experts take the PCIe lane (GPU arithmetic) depends on the whole window's misses, so a row's result depends on its neighbours.

## Timing
- Hardware: 2 × RX 7900 XTX 24 GB, 4 × Instinct MI50 16 GB (135 W cap), EPYC 7642 (48 cores), 256 GB DDR4-2666.
- Software: Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes on ROCm 7.2 (the runtime in Strata's ROCm image). Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Workload: 8 prompts × 384 tokens, temperature 0, presence 0; UD-IQ4_XS; with self-commit.
