# HW-14: A fifth MI50 fitted and qualified: the first heat soak stopped at 80 °C, the second passed after a cooling fix
Status: hardware event (card qualified; not yet a helper in a timed served run) · Date: 2026-11-17 (qualified 2026-11-19)
Code: no engine change. A qualification package (identity kernels, heat soak, P2P stale-read probe, log checks) that is not in this release.

## What and why
- On 17 Nov evening a fifth MI50 32 GB, taken from another of our machines, was fitted to the test machine. In these cards it is MI50 #5, the label HW-10's bench card used. The plan is to give the MI50s enough memory between them to take more experts off the CPU lane (FG-22).
- A card joins the helper set only after a qualification: bitwise identity against a production MI50, a 10-minute full-load soak with an abort at 80 °C junction, the P2P stale-read probe of HW-12, and clean kernel and PCIe error logs.

## Prediction
- None beyond passing the gates.

## Correctness
- Identity kernels (FMA, GEMM, FP16, integer mix, FP64), cold and hot: bitwise equal to a production MI50, self-consistent over 3 repetitions; memory pattern test 0 bad words (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 17–19 Nov, the test machine, the new card alone under load while the other cards were idle or serving their own runs. Junction and memory temperatures sampled every 2 s.

## Verdict
- **Qualified.** The new card is bitwise identical to a production MI50 and holds full load at 66 °C junction after the cooling fix.
- Not yet used as a helper in a timed served run. The five-helper runs that were waiting for it are queued.

## Reproduce

## Notes / caveats
