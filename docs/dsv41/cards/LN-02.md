# LN-02: Computing the CPU lane's experts from L3 cache: about twice as fast, "worth building" on the bench
Status: finding (worth building by its rule; not built; served gain likely much smaller) · Date: 2026-11-21
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only: a CPU-only bench using the lane's expert code unchanged). Flags: none.

## What and why
- The lane idles ~403 µs before each call on average. Idea (lane option 1): use that time to pre-load the guessed next-layer experts into L3, then compute from L3. Is L3 compute fast enough, and does the pre-load fit the idle window?

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: yes. From-L3 (SW) 75–120 µs an expert; from DRAM (SD) 165–200 µs; ratio r = SW/SD 0.45–0.7; the kernel from DRAM within 15 % of the 187 µs bandwidth figure; WORTH BUILDING if r ≤ 0.6 and the warm pass ≤ 300 µs.

## Correctness
- Not applicable (bench of the unchanged lane code).

## Timing
- 21 Nov, the test machine's CPU only (EPYC Zen 2, memory at 2,933 MT/s, HW-13), 15 threads, nice 19; a 1 GiB buffer of 57 MXFP4 experts of 18.80 MB; all 12 cells valid, repeats within 0.1 %.

## Results
| cell (1 activation) | µs an expert (mean) | label |
|---|---|---|
| kernel from DRAM (KD) | 177.2 (bandwidth figure 187) | MEASURED |

- Four of five predictions held; SD came in just under its band (161.8 vs ≥ 165) (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **Worth building by its rule: L3 halves the lane's per-expert compute, and the 148 µs warm pass fits the idle window.** The warm pass is a full-bandwidth DRAM read, so the saving exists only if the pre-load is hidden in idle time (warm + compute 225 µs > 162 µs from DRAM).
- Not built yet. The price is in frozen, drafter-off single-user units (lane 8.5 % of picks); served with the live cache the lane carries 0.5–2.2 % of picks, so the served gain is likely several times smaller (INFERRED). It overlaps live placement (LN-04), the guess hit rate is unmeasured, and four or eight users leave less idle time. Measure served lane time and the guess hit rate first.
