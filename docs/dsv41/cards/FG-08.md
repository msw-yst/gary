# FG-08: XTX kernel round: seven kernel candidates on the bench, one kept (`DS4_XK_QO`, build 14)
Status: kept (`DS4_XK_QO` in build 14; `DS4_XK_QA` later, in build 16; the other five dropped or bench-only) · Date: 2026-11-12
Code: `DS4_XK_QO` and, from build 16, `DS4_XK_QA` are in Gary's release commit; the five other candidates are not (internal branch only). Flags: `DS4_XK_QO=1` (kept); `DS4_XK_QA=1` (kept in build 16, addendum; default off); `DS4_XK_QX=1`, `DS4_XK_HCMIX=1`, `DS4_XK_ROUTE=1`, `DS4_XK_GATE=1`, `DS4_XK_ATTN=2` (default off; not kept).

## What and why
- A study of the XTX-side kernels on the lead's critical path (FEATURES.md §3, "pending analysis") named seven small kernel changes, each priced from a traced profile of build 12. Each was built behind its own flag and timed first on a single-card kernel bench (cold weights, exactness checked byte for byte), the order HX-23's lesson asked for. Only bench passers went to an engine A/B.
- `DS4_XK_QO` folds the int8 rounding of the attention output (o) into the kernel that finishes it, so one dependent launch a layer goes away. `DS4_XK_QX` feeds the experts' x from the int8 rows the hyper-connection fusion already makes. The others: a two-round hc_mix load (`HCMIX`), the router's table staged in LDS (`ROUTE`), more loads in flight in the gate (`GATE`), a different sparse-attention tile (`ATTN=2`), and a 32-row int8 epilogue for one GEMV (`QA`).

## Prediction
- Bench lines, each written before its run: HCMIX ≤ old − 4.0 µs; ROUTE lead ≤ old − 2.0 µs; GATE ≤ old − 1.0 µs and beyond 2 × IQR; ATTN ≤ old − 2.0 µs at 228 rows; QX the dropped launch ≥ 1.0 µs; QO ≤ old − 2.0 µs; QA exact.
- Engine, single session (B1): QO −0.10 to −0.20 ms a token (REPORTED from the run notes).
- Build 14 A/B (QO on build 13s): B1 −0.28 to −0.12 ms a token (central −0.20); production (served mix) −0.020 to +0.010 ms, i.e. about zero, because only a small share of production steps are one-row steps (below). Pre-registered: yes, in the build-14 prereg, before the run.

## Correctness
- Bench: every kernel byte-compared against the old one. The first form of `QA` differed by 1-64 ulp: the compiler fused a product the old kernel rounds separately; with the fused multiply-add pinned, 18 exactness runs were identical (REPORTED).
- Build 14 A/B: 4 identity lines IDENTICAL (unset vs build 13s; on vs off on decode, drafter decode and batch test), and the production token ids of the on and placebo arms identical to off in all 6 reps (MEASURED).
- `ATTN=2` was not taken further, so its exactness on the engine was not checked.

## Timing
- Bench: one XTX of the box, cold weights, 11 Nov afternoon. Engine arms: build 12 base, 11-12 Nov.
- Build 14 A/B: 12 Nov 22:07, pre-incident hardware (4 × RX 7900 XTX: XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8; 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W, Gen4 x4 each; XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV; MI50 DPM `high`). OFF / ON / placebo interleaved, 6 reps each; B1 = 64-token prompt, 256 tokens, drafter off; production = the served mix, matched by request position.

## Verdict
- **QO kept: exact, −0.15 ms a token in a single session, nothing measurable in serving.** Both results sit inside their pre-registered ranges. It is on in build 14 and build 15.
- **Why serving does not see it:** in the production harness only 16 of 314 decode steps (5.1 %) are one-row steps; the rest are drafter verify windows of 2–4 rows (REPORTED from the build-14 prereg). A one-row saving is worth about 5 % of its B1 size in serving [I]. Predictions for serving are now made from that row mix, not from B1.
- **The other six:** four failed their bench line, QX passed on the bench but saved nothing measurable in the engine (its launch is off the critical path in most layers [I]), and QA's fixed form passed but was not taken further in this round (it was later measured in the engine; see the addendum).
- An early production A/B of QO and QX printed −0.735 ms; it was an artefact: two reps forked at the first request's cancel timing. The harness was fixed and the production metric is now a request-matched mix (REPORTED).
