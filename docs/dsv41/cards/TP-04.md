# TP-04: New layout: 3 XTX at x16 + 3 MI50, every expert on a GPU
Status: kept · Date: 2026-11-08
Code: in Gary's release commit. Flags: `--tp 3 --helper-device <3 mi50>`.

## What and why
- Physical rebuild: three XTX on full x16 links and three MI50 helpers, so the 2.25 bpw pack fits entirely in GPU memory and the CPU lane goes to ~0.
- Target slice: the CPU lane and host-RAM expert reads, which dominated decode on the old layout (INFERRED).

## Prediction
- About 41 tok/s projected from the old layout's numbers.
- Pre-registered: yes (projection in the previous day's notes).

## Correctness
- Not run: no identity or quality comparison against the old layout (placement changed, so bits were expected to differ) (INFERRED).

## Timing
- Hardware: New layout: 3 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3, Gen4 x16) + 3 × MI50 32 GB helpers (MI50 #1–3, Gen4 x4 each); every routed expert on a GPU. XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- 3 decodes, median reported [M in the internal results note; not in the published extract].
