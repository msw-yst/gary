# AR-12: Faster host-route decode all-reduce variants: one 25–30 % faster in the bench at decode sizes, not yet ported
Status: parked (bench only; the port decision is open) · Date: 2026-11-18
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (bench variants on an internal branch). Flags: none (bench only).

## What and why
- Decode keeps the host-memory all-reduce (AR-10): 80 calls a decode step on the native build, about 32 µs each after the last card arrives (FG-21). With P2P ruled out at these sizes, two rounds of benches tried to make the host route itself cheaper.

## Prediction
- First round: one variant at 16–20 µs a call at the decode sizes (missed). Second round: a gain of at least 3.32 µs at T4 for the next variant.

## Verdict
- **Hpo is about 25–30 % faster than the current host route at decode sizes in the bench.** At about 80 calls a step that is worth roughly 0.5–0.8 ms a step if it carries over to serving (INFERRED). Why it is faster was not yet explained when the round ended, and it has not been ported or served.
- Hq does not clear its bar. The port-or-park decision is open.
