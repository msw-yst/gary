# DR-01: DSpark drafter settings: confidence 0.3, block 3
Status: kept · Date: 2026-11-08 (addendum 2026-11-19)
Code: in Gary's release commit. Flags: `--dspark-conf`, `--dspark-block` (the engine's options for running the drafter).

## What and why
- The drafter is DSpark, DeepSeek's own drafter, shipped with DeepSeek V4.1; Strata's engine runs it beside the model. This card tunes how the engine uses it on the new layout. Drafted output diverged from undrafted output; the divergence was traced to numerics from expert placement, not to the drafter.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- No quality gate was run on drafted vs undrafted output. The divergence explanation is reported, not tested (REPORTED).

- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.

## Addendum (2026-11-19): block 6 on the native pack
- On the native MXFP4 pack, block 6 lands about 5.2–5.5 tokens a drafted round against about 3.85 for block 3, and makes one user 11–12 % faster, bit-identical (DR-12; internal native build). It was held out of release 4 by its four-user no-harm bar.
- The drafter's confidence collapses on packs whose non-expert tensors are quantized: it offers full drafts in 27–31 % of rounds there against 90 % on native (DR-11).
