# NX-18: Reseating the slow-link MI50 (Gen1 x8 -> Gen3 x8)
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-22
Code: no engine change. Hardware: MI50 #3 reseated with the machine fully powered off.

## What and why
- MI50 #3's PCIe transfers cost 2.4-2.9x the other cards' while its kernels ran as fast (NX-14, "the slow-link MI50"). Reading the link state showed why: its root port had trained at 2.5 GT/s x8 (Gen1) on a port capable of Gen3 x8. Every other pool card's link was at its port's maximum.
- In the pool it holds about a quarter of the pool's experts, so each layer waits for its slowest round trip.

## Prediction
- No numeric prediction was recorded before the reseat.
- Pre-registered: no. The test that measured it (NX-17's window test) was pre-registered as a re-run after the reseat, with every arm re-measured.

## Correctness
- Not applicable (no engine change). Every arm after the reseat gave the reference text and the same speed-prompt texts as before it (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 2 × RX 7900 XTX 24 GB (272 W cap), 4 × Instinct MI50 16 GB (135 W cap), EPYC 7642 (48 cores), 256 GB DDR4-2666. Links after the reseat: MI50 #1 Gen4 x16, #2 Gen4 x8, #3 and #4 Gen3 x8.
- Workload: the pool at 8 slots per worker, 4+4 and 8+8 users, 384 tokens, temperature 0; the same four arms before (one trial) and after (two trials).

## Verdict
- After the reseat every arm and level was faster per agent: +5.1 to +10.3 %, median about +7 %. The link is the likely cause (INFERRED): the time of day and the engine build (the hugetlbfs arena change, NX-19, which does not touch the decode path) also changed between the two runs.
- Before any pool tuning, read every helper card's link state (`current_link_speed` / `current_link_width` of the card and its root port, or `lspci -vv` LnkSta) against what the port supports.

## Reproduce
- Read the link state of every GPU and its root port; reseat any card below its port's capability with the power fully removed (below); re-run a short pool A/B.

## Notes / caveats
- **Builders: remove AC power before changing or reseating a card.** On some server boards (one of ours among them) a soft power-off from the front button or the BMC keeps the standby rail and the PCIe slots powered; changing cards in that state can leave cards that fail to enumerate or train their links, and warm reboots do not clear it. Switch the supply off or unplug it, hold the power button for ~30 s to drain it, then change the card, and check enumeration and link state after the next boot.
- Only one trial before the reseat, and that run was interrupted by the power-off; it is reported because it was known before the re-run was pre-registered.
- An earlier idea, forcing a PCIe DPM level for this card, was dropped: the reseat fixed the link, and our DeepSeek engine work found manual DPM / link pinning neutral or worse.

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output), ESTIMATE (our arithmetic) or INFERRED (our own reading).
