# HX-44: MI50 expert-kernel bench round: split gate/up waves, gfx906 kernels for verify windows, a per-cell switch: none cleared its bar
Status: dropped (bench gates failed; no engine run) · Date: 2026-11-11
Code: not in the fork's release commit (an 11 Nov bench build whose kernel variants were never committed to the engine source; the bench program is `tests/dsv41/iq_helper_bench.cpp`). Flags: `DS4_GS=1` (+ `DS4_GS_TPB=4/6`), `DS4_NK=1` (+ `DS4_NK_BPC=8`), `DS4_MK_GU=1 DS4_MK_PH=1 DS4_MK_GU_TPB=5`, `DS4_G906_NEMAX=4` (reference arm).

## What and why
- The MI50 helper windows the lead waits for are mostly one- and two-expert windows. Before any engine time, a single-card bench timed variants of the gfx906 expert kernels against today's kernels on real expert bytes:
  - **one-row windows (T 1):** gate and up on separate waves (`DS4_GS`), at 4 or 6 tiles a block; the MK gate/up rewrite (HX-23) in another variant;
  - **verify windows (T 2–4):** the gfx906 machinery for 2–4-row windows, decoding each weight once for groups of up to 2 entries (`DS4_NK`), also at 8 blocks per CU; and, as a reference, sending all verify windows to the gfx906 kernels (`DS4_G906_NEMAX=4`).
- A follow-up on 12 Nov tried a switch that picks between today's path and the NEMAX=4 kernel per cell.

## Prediction
- One-row gate (gate B): a variant must beat today's kernel by ≥ 2.0 µs on the IQ2_XXS one-expert cell in both passes. `DS4_GS` predicted −1.5 to −5 µs (one expert) and −2 to −8 (two); TPB 4 and 6 and the MK variant 0 to +4.
- Verify gate (gate A): a mean ≤ −2.0 µs on one-decode cells and no cell of any type worse than +1.0 µs. `DS4_NK` predicted −3 to −12 µs on one-entry cells.
- Pre-registered: yes, in the bench script before the run.

## Correctness
- Every arm bit-identical to today's kernels in every cell, both passes (h and output BITWISE), every rc 0 (MEASURED).

## Timing
- One production MI50 (MI50 #4), one process an arm, 300 reps a cell, 2 passes; one-row cells re-timed in four later passes. 11 Nov. The NE switch follow-up: 12 Nov, early.

## Verdict
- **No variant cleared its bar, so none went to the engine.** `DS4_GS` came closest (−1.4 to −2.1 µs against −2.0) and is the only one that was faster at all.
- Why, as read at the time (INFERRED): one-expert gate/up is bound by latency, not by the number of units working; 144-block grids re-create the busiest SIMD's load and 96-block grids lose balance; the MK variant re-reads activations every step; `DS4_NK` spilled 11–20 dwords at 128 VGPRs plus LDS staging (the spill was read from the compiler output); denser grids are slower.
- NEMAX=4 wins one-entry verify groups and loses full ones. In production it was neutral (+0.07 ms, SV-09 / HX-23), and choosing per row group (HX-24) did not rescue it. The MI50 kernel levers moved to multi-row windows (HX-25).
