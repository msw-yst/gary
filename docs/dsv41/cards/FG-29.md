# FG-29: Decode attention split into 13 parts, then two bit-exact attention fixes: top-k −0.85 %, load-ahead attention +6 % slower; the peers wait for the lead at the MoE all-reduce
Status: finding (`DS4_TOPK_SMALL` small gain, not merged; `DS4_ATTN_PRE` dropped, FG-31) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branches only: attention timers, and the fixes built on them). Flags: `DS4_PROF_ATTN=8` (timers); `DS4_TOPK_SMALL=1`, `DS4_ATTN_PRE=1`, `DS4_AR_STAMP=1` (all-reduce stamps, diagnostic), `DS4_AF_SCHED` (switch them per decoded window, for an in-process A/B); all default off.

## What and why
- FG-25 found decode attention at 10.2 ms a token, flat from 2K to 8K. The attention profile splits that into its parts. The attention fixes then tried the two bit-exact candidates it suggested and stamped the all-reduce to see whether its cost is the call or the wait.
- `DS4_TOPK_SMALL=1`: the indexer's top-k over up to 8,192 scores in one 1,024-thread workgroup (histogram levels and block scans), checked at load against the old kernel byte for byte.
- `DS4_ATTN_PRE=1`: a decoded token's sparse attention takes the variant that issues its loads ahead (the CUDA builds' one-token kernel) instead of the HIP default; the same arithmetic in the same order, checked at load.

## Prediction
- Attention profile: none recorded (a survey).
- Attention fixes: a speed bar of 1.5 % for either fix; no numeric prediction recorded beyond that.

## Correctness
- Attention fixes: fixes on = off, 1,480 of 1,480 token ids identical (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 20 Nov, the test machine, 4 XTX + 5 MI50 helpers, the native MXFP4 pack on the stack build, frozen placement, greedy, drafter off, one user 2K (and 8K for the profile). The fixes were measured as an in-process ABBA, 16 blocks of 64 windows.

All-reduce stamps, µs a call (MEASURED):
- Attention all-reduce: own work ~11 + waiting for peers ~10. The profile's "~55 µs a call" was mostly the timing marks themselves.
- MoE all-reduce: the lead waits 2.2 µs; the other three XTX wait 110.4 / 110.2 / 108.7 µs a call (p50 ~58, p90 > 128). **Three of four XTX are idle about 4.4 ms a token waiting for the lead** (INFERRED from the per-call waits × 40 layers).
- Served drafter readout (drafter on, live placement, 1 user 2K): 103.2 / 110.2 tok/s; drafts kept 92–97 % at block 3 (position 1 98–100 %, 2 94–97 %, 3 87–98 %) (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **Attention's 10 ms is many small pieces, not one kernel:** no part is over 1.7 ms. The levers named then were the projection matvecs, the all-reduce, the sparse-attention kernel and fusing the small ops.
- **`DS4_TOPK_SMALL`: small and probably real (−0.85 %), under its bar.** It was not merged into the release candidates.
- **`DS4_ATTN_PRE`: slower, +6 %; dropped.** Why a bit-exact load-ahead kernel is slower was followed up in FG-31: the attention kernel itself is ~27 µs a layer slower untraced.
- **New top lever at the time: the lead's lateness at the MoE all-reduce** (the other cards wait ~110 µs a call). That started the side-stream work of FG-30; later the cause was traced to the CPU lane answering late (FG-33).
- High draft acceptance at block 3 suggested a longer drafting block.
