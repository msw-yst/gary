# QA-15: A mostly-native pack (65 % of experts native MXFP4, the rest 2-bit) misses its quality bar: KL 0.0118 against 0.010; the all-3-bit pack shelved
Status: dropped (quality FAIL; speed phase not run) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only: mixed per-expert precision, QA-12). Measurement: teacher-forced KL against the hosted model's log-probabilities (QA-11).

## What and why
- On the native pack the experts that fit on no GPU go to the CPU lane (FG-22). A pack with most experts native and the coldest at 2 bits could fit every expert on a GPU and empty the lane, if the quality holds.
- x65: 10,000 of 15,744 experts (the drafter's included) native MXFP4, chosen by how often they are picked; the rest at the 2.25-bit pack's IQ2_XXS. Five helpers.

## Prediction
- Pre-registered bar: KL from the hosted model ≤ 0.010; then speed against native with five helpers. x60 and x55 were to follow only if x65 passed.

## Correctness
- Placement check PASS: 3,640 native experts in the XTX pools plus 631 2-bit ones on the XTXs; each helper about 2,218–2,223 experts (about 30.9 GiB); about 384 experts (2.4 %) still off the GPUs; smallest free VRAM 460 MiB (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 20 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 4) and 5 × MI50 helpers. Same prompts, positions and scorer as QA-11.

## Verdict
- **FAIL.** x65 closes about 85 % of the gap between the 2.25-bit pack and native, but misses the 0.010 bar by 18 %, and it still leaves about 2.4 % of the experts off the GPUs, so it does not quite empty the CPU lane either.
- x60 and x55 were not run: their native sets are subsets of x65's, so they cannot do better (INFERRED). The speed phase was skipped.
- **The all-3-bit pack is shelved.** A whole-model 3-bit pack would fit every expert on the GPUs by about 1 % (QA-16), but with five helpers the CPU lane is only about 4.5 % of a decode token (FG-25), so emptying it is worth little against a certain quality cost. The native 4-bit pack stays the product.
- The lever, if this is revisited, is the cold tier's format and how it was made (QA-16), not the share of native experts.
