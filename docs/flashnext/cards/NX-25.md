# NX-25: MI50 Q4_K prompt-expert kernel rate, and the planned MI50 prompt helper
Status: finding (kernel rate) + plan (helper not built) · Date: 2026-11-22
Code: the Q4_K cases and the HIP target of `mmq_expert_bench` (`STRATA_BENCH_ONLY=q4_k` picks them). The prompt helper does not exist yet; its planned switch is `STRATA_PREFILL_POOL_HELP=1`.

## What and why
- On the pool, prompt reading is the limit for agent turns ([NX-22](NX-22.md)). A read on a worker lends half of its XTX's hot expert slots and streams every expert the read picks that the XTX does not keep, ~23,000 of 24,576 (~64 GiB) per part, whatever the read's length. Measured single-part reads: 353 new tokens 2.29 s, ~1,000 3.1 s, 1,848 3.65 s, 7,869 5.39 s, so about 2.2 s of every read is that stream (ESTIMATE from the times).
- Every one of those experts already sits in an MI50's VRAM (the pool holds 21,426, the XTX 3,150, the CPU none). An MI50 prompt helper would compute them where they are and remove the stream, rather than adding work beside it.
- First question: is the MI50's Q4_K prompt arithmetic fast enough?

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: no (a desk study with one kernel measurement).

## Correctness
- Not applicable to the measurement (synthetic weights, timing only).
- The helper would change the experts' arithmetic (gfx906 kernels, another summation order), so it will not be bit-identical to today's path. The plan gates it on quality instead: teacher-forced over 256 tokens on 48 fixed prompts, argmax agreement >= 99.0 % and mean KL <= 0.003 against helper-off, with the XTX path's own run-to-run noise measured beside it. It will not be called exact.

## Timing
- Hardware: one Instinct MI50 16 GB on Gen4 x16, DPM auto (no pin).
- Workload: `mmq_expert_bench`, llama.cpp's MMQ products at the engine's shapes (n_embd 2560, n_ff 640), 16 experts a launch, median of 25, synthetic weights.

Per prompt token, the MI50s' share (~72 % of 480 expert rows, over four cards) is ~0.07-0.12 ms of kernel time a card, and ~0.6 MB of PCIe traffic a card (~0.1 ms on the Gen3 x8 cards) (ESTIMATE).

## Verdict
- The MI50 arithmetic is not the limit: 7-12 int8-TOPS at 12-160 rows an expert.
- **Planned, not built, not a claim:** a helper-side prompt entry on each MI50 over the experts its cache holds, and a prompt-path split with no loan and no stream for a helped part. ESTIMATE: warm agent-turn reads 3.1-3.65 s -> ~0.3-0.6 s, a cold 8K read 5.4 s -> ~1.5-2.5 s, and the agent-turn headline 2 -> 4 agents (8 agents stay at the decode limit, steady 4+4 = 32 tok/s). Every number here must be measured served before it counts.
- Plan, one step at a time: (1) the helper entry and a parity / precision test against the XTX path; (2) the prompt-path split behind `STRATA_PREFILL_POOL_HELP=1`, with a read-length cap; (3) a served read-time A/B with the quality gate above; (4) agent turns at 4, 8 and 16 agents.

## Reproduce
- Build `mmq_expert_bench` for gfx906 and run it on one MI50 with the engine's shapes.

## Notes / caveats
- Our file's down projection is Q5_0; the measured build has no Q5_0 MMQ instance, so the Q4_K and Q8_0 down rows bracket it.
- Prior art: our DeepSeek engine's MI50 prompt helper lost while the XTX still streamed experts no helper held, and won once nothing else streamed. The pool streams nothing the helpers do not hold, which is why we expect the second case. It was also slower when served from 16K tokens up, cause unknown, hence the read-length cap.
- Decode on the MI50s: in a steady window ~1/3 of the time is the wait for the helpers (9.6-9.9 of 29.6 ms at 3.7 rows, 15-18 of 44-48 ms at 7-8 rows; MEASURED). A faster MI50 Q4_K / Q5_0 decode kernel is the lever there (ESTIMATE: 4+4 from 32 to ~36 per agent, not 40).

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output), ESTIMATE (our arithmetic) or INFERRED (our own reading).
