# AR-07: Decode all-reduce levers on build 10: FUSE_NORM (FN) and OVERLAP (OV)
Status: shelved · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: `DS4_AR_OVERLAP` is in the fork's release commit (default off); `DS4_AR_FUSE_NORM` is not (internal branch only).

## What and why
- FN: fuse the post-all-reduce norm into the reduce. OV: overlap the all-reduce with the next kernels on a second stream.

## Prediction
- +3.5–7 % at B1.
- Pre-registered: yes (notes before the run).

## Correctness
- FN vs off: IDENTICAL on all batch tests (MEASURED).
- OV vs off: DIFFER on every rep, tp3h3 and tp4h4 (a known bug with the drafter) (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- B1/B2/B8, 3 reps, interleaved, fresh process per arm.
