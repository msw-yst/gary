# PF-06: MI50 prefill helper
Status: dropped (not adopted) · Date: 2026-11-08 (addendum 2026-11-19)
Code: inclusion in the release commit to be confirmed in the rename / build phase. Flags: (set requests).

## What and why
- Let the MI50s compute lightly used helper-held experts during the chunked prompt instead of streaming them to the XTX.

## Prediction
- Stop if an MI50 reached 75 °C.
- Pre-registered: yes.

## Correctness
- Parity bitwise unless an expert is split; chunked score within noise for stripe placement (REPORTED).

## Timing
- Hardware: New layout: 3 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3, Gen4 x16) + 3 × MI50 32 GB helpers (MI50 #1–3, Gen4 x4 each); every routed expert on a GPU. XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Reported from notes at the time; the raw runs are not in the published extract.

## Verdict
- Not adopted: the XTX path was faster than what the MI50s could add.

## Reproduce
- Gary's release commit with the flags above; a scripted public re-run has not been prepared for this card.

## Notes / caveats
- Reported, not reproduced.

## Addendum (2026-11-19): the MI50 prompt helper came back, and on the native pack it now wins
- The idea returned as PF-11 (2.25-bit pack, served from build 13s) and, on the native MXFP4 pack, as PF-14, PF-18, PF-19 and PF-20 (internal native build, not in a release).
- On native the helper first made prompts slower (PF-14, PF-18): the MI50s took the hot experts that were already in host RAM, and what was left to send to the XTXs was the cold, disk-backed part, in small copy batches.
- With the RAM store holding only what the XTXs must stream, full batches, and DMA straight from the store, a native 2K prompt's first token went from 15.2 to 3.3 s, with the MI50s' own work fully hidden (PF-19, PF-20).
