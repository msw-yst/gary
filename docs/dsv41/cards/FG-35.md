# FG-35: Is the host the one-user decode bottleneck? No: it runs 15–18 ms ahead of the GPU and is never starved
Status: finding (refutes our host-headroom reading of FG-30) · Date: 2026-11-21
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal measurement branch only). Flags: `DS4_HOSTROOM=1` (measurement only, default off): per decoded window, each card thread's host clock and a GPU event at its first launch, after one sampled layer, and after its last launch; plus the forward's host phases and head wait. Arms switched with `DS4_AF_SCHED`.

## What and why
- Under a kernel trace, the side-stream changes of FG-30 and the load-ahead attention kernel (FG-29) looked neutral or faster; untraced they were 6–11 % slower. We read that as: they add host API time and eat the host's run-ahead, so untraced (GPU faster) the host becomes the critical path.
- This run tests that reading without a tracer: per token, how long each card's launch loop takes, how far submission runs ahead of completion (slack), and whether the GPU ever waits for the host.

## Prediction
- H-host (our reading): a large share of each feature's slowdown is host-attributable, with lost slack and starved samples. Bar: host-attributable delta / wall delta ≥ 0.70.

## Correctness
- All arms identical to off (EXACT PASS) (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **The host is never the one-user bottleneck.** In every arm it spends 3–4 ms a token launching and finishes 15–18 ms before the GPU; no sample shows the GPU waiting for it. H-host is NOT SUPPORTED: our inference from the traced arms of FG-30 was wrong.
- The slowdowns are on the GPU and appear only untraced: the GPU span grows by the full delta. Stage timing then located them: the attention kernel itself for ATTN_PRE, a late publish to the MI50s for ROUTE (FG-31).
- It also means that launch cost is not what limits one-user decode, which agrees with the graph-capture results (FG-32).

## Notes / caveats
- The speed deltas reproduce FG-30's and FG-31's within their noise.
- Why the tracer hides real GPU-side costs is not settled; the leading candidate is that it serialises or re-times concurrent streams and cross-card work (INFERRED).
