# SV-09: Build 12: the exact overnight flags merged and measured as one stack
Status: kept (build 12 = F + LP + HW + Engram split) · Date: 2026-11-11
Code: in Gary's release commit. Flags on top of build 11's: `DS4_AR_POST=3 DS4_ATTN_MERGE=2` (F, FG-07); `DS4_AHEAD=0 DS4_LP_PUBFUSE=1 DS4_LP_JOINCOMB=1 DS4_LP_FLAGFUSE=1` (LP, HX-21); `DS4_HW_FOLD=1 DS4_HW_HEADQ8=1 DS4_HW_PUBT=1` (HW, HX-22); `DS4_EG_SPLIT=1`. Measured and left off: `DS4_MK_GU/DN` (HX-23), `DS4_G906_NEMAX=4`, `DS4_DSPARK_ADAPT=1`, `DS4_DSPARK_NOSEED=1`.

## What and why
- Every exact flag from the night's work went into one binary, each default off, so they could be gated together and measured as a cumulative stack: F, then + LP, then + HW, then + MK. The fastest stack was then tried with two smaller items on top:
  - `DS4_EG_SPLIT=1`: the Engram layers' 108 MB GEMV (129 µs on every XTX, replicated) is split by output rows over the four XTX and gathered by an all-reduce. Exact because every other member contributes −0.0, the exact additive identity in round-to-nearest.
  - `DS4_DSPARK_NOSEED=1`: with `--dspark off`, the engine still fed the drafter after every token (a 101 µs projection and three small KV writes, queued in front of the sampler). This flag skips that when the process can never draft. It changes drafter-off timing only; production runs with the drafter on, where the feeding is needed.
- A production phase then served eight long prompts (3.8K–60.6K tokens, read cold) with the drafter on and the benchmark's sampling (temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20), 1,024 tokens each.

## Verdict
- **The stack is exact and faster everywhere it was measured.** B1 −7.0 % with F + LP + HW, −7.7 % with the Engram split on top (14.38 → 13.27 ms, 69.5 → 75.3 tok/s on this prompt); B8 −6.8 %, well clear of the rep spread; greedy drafter-on +4.6 % (one run); production −4.4 to −5.3 % a generated token at matched output length.
- **Build 12 = F + LP + HW + `DS4_EG_SPLIT=1`**, the configuration chosen for serving. MK (slower, HX-23), NEMAX 4 (no gain) and ADAPT (below) are off. NOSEED only affects drafter-off processes, so it is not part of the serving configuration; it does confirm that every drafter-off number in these cards carries about 0.3 ms of unused drafter feeding, on both sides of each comparison.
- **Against the predictions:** F held (−0.699 in −0.60 to −0.73). F + LP held (−0.932 in −0.75 to −1.00). F + LP + HW landed at the low edge (−1.009 in −0.85 to −1.25). MK was disproved (HX-23). EG_SPLIT held (−0.122 in −0.06 to −0.17). NOSEED landed at the top of its range (−0.300). Production matched the central prediction (−0.55 ms, −5 %). B8 beat its range (−2.8 against −0.8 to −2.0).
- **The production summary's means are misleading and are not used.** The sampled serve runs produced different output lengths from rep to rep (629 or 1,626 tokens for the same arm and seed), and short outputs run faster per token. Means over mixed reps compare unlike with unlike; only length-matched reps are compared above. The summary's "−11 %" for ADAPT is that artefact: all three ADAPT reps happened to be short, and length-matched it equals build 12. It is not a gain.
- The production comparison is two long reps and one short rep a side. Its spread is narrow at matched length, but it is a small sample.

## Notes / caveats
- The B1 runs use a 64-token prompt. The build-11 headline (65.16 tok/s, SV-06) used a 2,048-token prompt; on that prompt, in this run's single identity runs, the build-11 binary read 64.70 tok/s and F + LP + HW 69.41 tok/s (EG_SPLIT was not in a 2K run on its own).
- The production prompts top out at 60.6K tokens and are read cold; live agent traffic averages ~55–66K tokens of context with ~95 % prefix reuse (SV-08).
- B1 and the best-stack phase were measured in separate phases of the same run (13.375 and 13.394 for the same stack), which bounds the drift between phases at about 0.02 ms.
- Addendum (2026-11-13): builds 13, 13s, 14 and 15 followed (SV-10, FG-08, SV-14); each kept build 12's flags and was gated for identity against its predecessor. Of the items measured and left off here, `DS4_DSPARK_ADAPT` was later tried at its own design point and dropped (DR-08).
