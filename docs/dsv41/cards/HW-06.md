# HW-06: The "Gen3" misreading was idle PCIe power saving
Status: corrected · Date: 2026-11-10 (addendum 2026-11-13, replaced 2026-11-14; addendum 2026-11-19)
Code: —. Flags: —.

## What and why
- We wanted to know whether an MI50's round trip changes when its Gen4 x4 link runs at Gen3 x4. To test it, the link was retrained at runtime from the root port.

## Prediction
- None recorded; it was a question. Safety lines: the link must read 8.0 GT/s x4 after retraining; any AER or link error triggers a revert and stop; the revert must return to 16 GT/s.
- Pre-registered: safety lines only.

## Correctness
- The "before" reading was already 2.5 GT/s x4. After retraining to Gen3, and after each revert, it still read 2.5 GT/s (MEASURED). The script stopped; no engine ran. No AER or driver errors (MEASURED).
- It was first reported as a link stuck at Gen1.
- After a full power cycle, all four MI50 links read 2.5 GT/s x4 at idle; `pp_dpm_pcie` lists level 0 = 2.5 GT/s x4 and level 1 = 16 GT/s x4. The card's SMU drops the link at idle and raises it under load; a later run logged the switch under load (REPORTED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `high` (CP-03).
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Not applicable; nothing was timed.

## Verdict
- Nothing was stuck or damaged. The method read link speed on an idle card. The Gen3 question is still unmeasured.

## Reproduce
- Proposed: read the link while the helper is under load. Do not use the manual DPM mode, which froze clocks in CP-02. About 30–40 GPU-min. (Done for decode on 14 Nov; see the addendum.)

## Notes / caveats
- Absolute numbers come from a private prompt set (not published); a re-run on a public text is pending. Ratios between arms of the same run are the comparable quantity.

## Addendum (2026-11-14, replaces the 13 Nov addendum): under load at `high` the links hold Gen4; pinning gains nothing
- The 13 Nov addendum said the MI50 links switched between Gen1 and Gen4 while a decode test ran. That reading was wrong in its main claim: the Gen1 samples came from idle moments between engine runs, not from decode itself [I].
- The test: build 15, MI50s at the production DPM `high` profile, alternating arms C (link free, as deployed) and P (link pinned at level 1, 16 GT/s x4) in the order C P P C C P P C, then a placebo L (P's manual mode with the link left free) and a closing C. Each arm ran the batch test at B1 and B8 (drafter off) while `pp_dpm_pcie` and each root port's current link speed were sampled (53–56 samples an arm). Pre-registered: B1 −0.1 ms a step (0 to −0.4), B8 −0.3 ms (0 to −1.0), served 32K first token −5 % (0 to −20 %).
- **Under load at `high`, the links stayed at Gen4 for 100 % of every decode arm** (C and P alike, all four cards, both readings), so the run had no contrast (MEASURED, pcie-pin.txt).
- **Pinning made no difference:** B1 C 14.562 / P 14.577 ms a step (+0.016, noise 0.030); B8 C 38.97 / P 38.71 ms (−0.26, noise 0.56); both inside the noise (MEASURED, pcie-pin.txt).
- **When the links were left free to drop under load** (placebo L), they ran at Gen4 only 48–61 % of the time and B8 decode took 45.6 ms a step against 38.9 ms for C, 17 % slower (B1 unchanged, 14.53 ms) (MEASURED, one arm, pcie-pin.txt). This is consistent with link speed mattering for batched decode; L also used the manual DPM mode, which was slower on its own in CP-02, so the two causes are not separated by this arm [I].
- The served long-prompt half was not measured: the server's own start changed the MI50 power profile, which the run's safety check treats as invalid, so it stopped at its first switch; all four cards were restored to `auto` with their 135 W caps (MEASURED, pcie-pin.txt).
- **So:** production does not drop the links under decode load, and nothing in this release needs a link pin. The served long-prompt case and the Gen3 question remain unmeasured.

## Addendum (2026-11-19): a link left at Gen1 after idle
- A platform check on 18 Nov found an MI50 link still at Gen1 after idle in 4 of 64 tests (0.91 against 7.14 GB/s at 64 MiB) (MEASURED, HW-15).
- While serving, the MI50 links carry about 1 % of their capacity (HW-15), so no serving cost is expected (INFERRED). It matters for bulk transfers to an MI50 that has been idle.
