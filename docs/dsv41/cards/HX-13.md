# HX-13: Diagnostic: why the tp4 helper wait is longer (slow card or TP width?)
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: in the fork's release commit. Flags: none.

## What and why
- On tp4 the lead's helper wait at B8 was longer than on tp3. Two explanations: XTX #4 (on a x8 link) is slow, or wider TP changes the helper traffic pattern. Swap XTX #4 into tp3 and see.

## Prediction
- Reading rules written before the run: if swapping XTX #4 in raises the wait, blame the card; if not, blame TP width.
- Pre-registered: yes (run script reading rules).

## Correctness
- Not applicable (diagnostic).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
