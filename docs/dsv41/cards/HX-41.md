# HX-41: Moving experts from the XTXs to the MI50s makes serving slower; a fresh hotness ranking is mixed
Status: finding (placement unchanged; the fresh ranking not adopted) · Date: 2026-11-16
Code: in the fork's release commit (no new code; launch options only). Flags: `--vram-experts F` (share of each XTX's expert budget; default 1.0), `--expert-profile FILE` (which experts count as hot).

## What and why
- The MI50s were not full when served: each held about 2,230 experts and had room for about 2,957, about 44 GiB free across the four (REPORTED from the prep notes). The question asked: does giving the MI50s more of the decode work shorten a served step?
- Two launch options already move the split, with no build:
  - `--vram-experts` lowers how many experts the XTXs hold; the MI50s take the rest.
  - `--expert-profile` re-ranks which experts are hot, and so which ones stay on the XTXs.
- Four arms on build 17:
  - **C0:** build 17 as served.
  - **R:** a fresh ranking built from a night of served agent traffic.
  - **X85:** `--vram-experts 0.865`, about 85 % of today's XTX trunk experts.
  - **X70:** `--vram-experts 0.731`, about 70 %.

## Prediction
- The hypothesis under test: more MI50 work shortens the step. Our written estimate before the run predicted the opposite, for these reasons:
  - the helper finishes after the lead's join in 99 % of layers;
  - its chain is about 76 µs of mostly fixed latency;
  - with `DS4_HELPER_DD_SKIP`, more MI50 picks means more layers that wait.
- Estimate: shifting +0.1 to +0.2 ms a token slower at one user; the fresh ranking about −0.3 ms a token (about −4 %), halving the MI50 share of decode picks to about 12 %.
- Manipulation check (MI50 share of picks): R < C0 < X85 < X70. Pre-registered: yes, before the run (the X-arm identity check was fixed in a pre-run addendum before the re-run).

## Correctness
- Identity: C0 and R outputs identical to build 17's reference in the first run (MEASURED). X85 and X70 move experts to a different card and kernel, so they are checked for determinism and placement, not for identity with C0 [I].
- Manipulation, on the identity prompt: XTX experts C0 7,172, X85 6,150, X70 5,143 (trunk 0.849 and 0.701 of C0's, inside their bands) (REPORTED from the package addendum). MI50 share of trunk picks: C0 20.1 %, X85 25.5 %, X70 30.0 %, R 43.4 % (MEASURED). CPU lane 0 in every arm.
- R's 43.4 % against a predicted ~12 % (band 5–18 %) is not a bug. The ranking follows the night's traffic. On that traffic, held out by time, it gives 8.8 % against the pack profile's 26.2 %. But the identity prompt falls into a ~60-token loop whose experts the night ranks cold, so R puts them on the MI50s (REPORTED from the package addendum).

## Verdict
- **Moving experts onto the MI50s makes every cell slower** (one user +0.37 to +0.46 ms a token, four and eight users −2 to −5 %). The join wait grows with each expert moved: with today's helper path every expert moved to an MI50 costs more than it frees. Our pre-run estimate (slower) held; the hypothesis under test is refuted.
- **The fresh ranking is mixed:** slower at 2K (+0.21 ms), faster at 8K (−0.13 ms) and on first token. It ranks a different workload than the test prompts, so this run cannot make it a deploy candidate. It was not adopted; served traffic would have to measure it.
- Placement stays as it is. The MI50 lever is a cheaper helper round trip, not more MI50 work (the MI50 kernel, HX-36).

## Reproduce
- Serve build 17 with `--vram-experts 0.865` / `0.731` and compare one-user and multi-user decode against the default. The night-traffic profile is not published.

## Notes / caveats
- **First run (16 Nov 14:22): NO DATA.** R measured +0.19 ms a token, and the X85 / X70 arms were dropped. A package bug read the prompt helper's "40 of 40 layers" line as the arms' expert count, so the check saw no change. The engine and the option were fine. Fixed and re-run the same afternoon; the re-run is the table above (REPORTED from the package addendum).
- The verdict at the time proposed making the round trip cheaper first with graph launch plus zero-copy (105 → 69 µs). That change was already the default (since 7 Nov). The 105 µs was a microbench of the old path (correction of 17 Nov, HX-36).
- R's 2K and 8K deltas are resolved, but R ranks a different workload than the test, so they say little about a ranking built from served traffic [I].
