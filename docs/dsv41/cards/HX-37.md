# HX-37: A fifth MI50 helper on the native pack: one user 10 % faster, four users 10 % more, the CPU lane's share halved
Status: kept (GO; five helpers are the base of every later native run; internal native build, not in a release) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: no new code. The fifth MI50 (HW-14) is added as a helper by command line (`--helper-device`), on the internal native build with PF-20's prompt switches.

## What and why
- On the native MXFP4 pack the experts that fit on no GPU are computed by the CPU lane, and the lead XTX waits for it (FG-21, FG-22). A fifth MI50 gives the helpers about 30 GB more room, so fewer experts are left to the lane.
- The card was qualified on 19 Nov (HW-14). This run asks whether it pays as a helper: four helpers (A) against five (F) on the full new prompt setup (PF-20), and a report-only pair on the older production settings (P4, P5).

## Prediction
- Pre-registered gates: the CPU lane's share of decode expert picks falls (gate M1), drift within 0.97–1.03 for both arms, text closeness of F against A. The speed bars are not restated here.

## Correctness
- Text closeness, F1 against A1: 4 of 4 close (MEASURED). Placement made deterministic with `DS4_CACHE_DET=1` (ME-13).
- Not a bit-identity test: a different helper count is a different placement, so the outputs are not expected to be identical (QA-08).

## Timing
- 19–20 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 4) and 4 or 5 × MI50 32 GB helpers at 135 W; one EPYC Zen 2 16-core host, DDR4 at 2933 (HW-13). Native MXFP4 pack. Order A1 F1 F2 A2, then P4 P5 (one start each). Locked placement for the A/F arms (see ME-16 for what that means for speed).

## Verdict
- **GO.** On the new prompt setup the fifth helper is worth about 10 % on one-user decode and 10 % on four users, with the first token 12 % lower at 2K and within 2 % at 8K–16K. On the older settings the gain is about 4 % (one start each, report only).
- The CPU lane now carries about 3 % of the expert picks. With five helpers the lane is a small part of a decode token (4.5 %, FG-25), which is why a smaller all-3-bit pack to empty it was shelved (QA-15).
