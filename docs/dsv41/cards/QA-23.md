# QA-23: The community 3-bit pack (UD-IQ3_S experts) fails the quality bar, at about a quarter of the predicted loss; an all-IQ3_XXS pack was shelved
Status: dropped (fails the quality bar; not a ship candidate under it) · Date: 2026-11-22
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only, the loader of QA-22, built in the run; control on release candidate r2a). Flags: none (the pack selects the expert types).

## What and why
- QA-22 made the published UD-IQ3_S pack's experts loadable. Its experts are 236 GiB against our native 269 GiB, which moves about 1,700 experts off the CPU lane. The question: how far do KL and top-1 move when the routed experts are UD-IQ3_S instead of native MXFP4, with everything else (dense weights, Engram, drafter) byte-identical to the served pack?
- Only the IQ3_S tensors were fetched (101.7 GB; disk room), so the IQ3_XXS arms were dropped from this run.

## Prediction
- dKL +0.006 to +0.015 (central about +0.010), dTOP1 −1 to −4 pp (INFERRED from the bit count). Pre-registered: yes. Bar as QA-20: dKL ≤ +0.001 AND dTOP1 ≥ −0.3 pp; FAIL when the whole 95 % interval is beyond either.

## Correctness
- Exactness gate: the mixed-type build on the served native pack equals r2a at all 2,900 positions (KL, argmax and NLL differ at 0) (MEASURED). The end-of-run control repeats it exactly (MEASURED).
- Engagement: the IQ3_S arm logged its mixed layers (the manifest's 18 mixed layers: 0–9, 20–24, 35–37) (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 22 Nov, the test machine (4 XTX, 5 MI50 helpers). QA-11's 12 prompts, 2,900 positions, teacher-forced along hosted's greedy continuations; paired two-level bootstrap (10,000 resamples). Quality only; speed not measured.

## Verdict
- **FAIL against both controls**: dKL about 2.75× the +0.001 limit, top-1 −1.3 pp. Not a ship candidate under the current bar.
- It is far better than predicted: about a quarter of the central predicted loss, and about 7 % of the 2.25-bit pack's distance from native (0.00275 against 0.0381, INFERRED arithmetic). A credible option for a smaller box if the bar were relaxed for fits with fewer cards.
- Decision (20 Nov): an all-IQ3_XXS pack was **shelved**, with native 4-bit staying the product: the CPU lane was then put at only about 3 % of decode (REPORTED), so a pack whose main gain is emptying the lane was not worth its quality risk. (QA-22 later found that the published UD-IQ3_XXS does not fit anyway; a uniform IQ3_XXS would only just fit.)

## Reproduce
- Not reproducible from the release: the mixed-type loader is on an internal build. Pack: our packer's routed-expert override on top of the served native pack, with the UD-IQ3_S low-bit tensors. Quality method: `validation/hostlp/` (QA-11).

## Notes / caveats
- One run an arm; the controls are bit-reproducible, so the interval is the prompt-and-position sampling, not run noise.
- "3-bit" is the pack's name: its experts are a mix (about 60 % of expert bytes stay MXFP4, the rest Q3_K, IQ3_XXS, IQ2_S and IQ3_S; QA-22).
- Speed and agent-task quality (task outcomes rather than KL) were not measured.
