# CP-06: XTX performance level `high`: half the P2P hand-off in the lab, nothing measurable served, +218 W at idle
Status: dropped (not adopted) · Date: 2026-11-18
Code: no engine change. A driver setting (the XTXs' power performance level), applied for the run and rolled back.

## What and why
- HW-15 found that an idle receiving XTX doubles the P2P hand-off and caps P2P bandwidth at about 15 GB/s. Forcing the XTXs' performance level to `high` keeps their clocks up. This run measures what that buys in the lab and in serving, and what it costs at idle.

## Prediction
- No numeric prediction was recorded. HW-15's latency probe had measured 1.28–1.44 µs with both cards' clocks up.

## Correctness
- Overdrive and fan settings checked before and after: 14 checks unchanged (MEASURED). No output-changing switch.

## Timing
- 18 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX + 4 × MI50; lab probes, then one short served pair (8K first token, one-user decode).

## Verdict
- **Not adopted.** The lab gains are real at small messages, but the served cells show nothing that stands out of their own noise, and the four cards draw 218 W more at idle.
- The run's own verdict line is FAIL (a configuration check on two arms); the served pair is too noisy (n = 3, spread 21–29 %) to resolve a gain of this size (ME-14).
- While serving, the XTX clocks are already at full (HW-15), which explains why forcing them up changes little there (INFERRED).

## Reproduce
- Set the XTXs' power performance level to `high` through sysfs, time a flag hand-off between two cards, and read the idle power; restore `auto`.
