# AR-03: Peer-to-peer decode all-reduce
Status: dropped (NO-GO) · Date: 2026-11-08 (addendum 2026-11-19)
Code: not in Gary's release commit: no switch named `DS4_AR_P2P` is in its code. Flags: `DS4_AR_P2P` (opt-in, on the branch measured here).

## What and why
- Do the decode all-reduce with XTX-to-XTX peer writes instead of through host memory.

## Prediction
- +20–30 %.
- Pre-registered: yes.

## Correctness
- Bit-identical (REPORTED).

## Timing
- Hardware: New layout: 3 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3, Gen4 x16) + 3 × MI50 32 GB helpers (MI50 #1–3, Gen4 x4 each); every routed expert on a GPU. XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Reported from notes at the time; the raw runs are not in the published extract.

## Verdict
- Wrong, badly. The prediction was computed against a stale baseline in which the all-reduce was a larger share of the token than it was by then (1.3–1.5 ms per token) (REPORTED). Logged in the error table (ME-07).

## Reproduce
- Gary's release commit with the flags above; a scripted public re-run has not been prepared for this card.

## Notes / caveats
- Absolute numbers come from a private prompt set (not published); a re-run on a public text is pending. Ratios between arms of the same run are the comparable quantity.

## Addendum (2026-11-19): why it lost
- Measured on 18 Nov (AR-10, HW-15): at decode sizes a write lands in host memory about 1 µs sooner than in a peer XTX's memory; one card writing small uncached pieces to three peers gets a fifth of its pair bandwidth; an idle receiving card doubles the hand-off. Fences, the board and the firmware were ruled out.
- Direct P2P wins only at prompt sizes (AR-11).
