# HX-07: Stripe placement of helper experts (default in build 8)
Status: kept · Date: 2026-11-08
Code: in the fork's release commit. Flags: `DS4_HELPER_PLACE=stripe` (default; `layer` selectable).

## What and why
- Spread each layer's helper experts across all MI50s (rank r on helper r mod n) instead of giving each MI50 whole layers, so the helpers work in parallel within a layer.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- Regression identical with the flag at its old value (REPORTED).

## Timing
- Hardware: New layout: 3 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3, Gen4 x16) + 3 × MI50 32 GB helpers (MI50 #1–3, Gen4 x4 each); every routed expert on a GPU. XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Reported from notes at the time; the raw runs are not in the published extract.
