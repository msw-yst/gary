# TP-11: Today's engine on two XTX without helpers: 29.6–34 tok/s one user, 39 tok/s four users; the other arms were invalid
Status: finding (one valid arm; the rest not measured because of a harness bug) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only). Layout by command line (`--tp 2`), on the internal native build.

## What and why
- Do the optimisations help on fewer cards? Planned arms: two XTX alone, two XTX + four MI50 helpers (old and new configuration, locked and live), one XTX + four or five helpers.
- Desk checks first: tensor-parallel 1 and 2 load; one XTX fits only with the drafter off and a 32K context; two XTX lose the peer-to-peer all-reduce paths (they need three or more members); two XTX without helpers must stream most experts, 289 GB of them against 251 GB of host RAM.

## Prediction
- Two XTX without helpers: bound by RAM and disk, far slower than four XTX + five helpers.

## Correctness
- Speed only. The only valid arm passed its configuration gate (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 20 Nov, the test machine: 2 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 2), no MI50 helpers; native MXFP4 pack; locked placement.

## Verdict
- **Two XTX without helpers decode about 3.5× slower than four XTX with five helpers** (33.8 against 9.7 ms a token, both locked), and are bound by streaming experts from RAM and disk, as predicted.
- **Every other arm is invalid, from a harness bug, not a machine fault.** The "old configuration" arms inherited the placement-freeze setting from the new configuration's environment; our configuration gate refused them, and each chain stopped there. The two-XTX-with-helpers and one-XTX arms did not produce data. They are owed.
