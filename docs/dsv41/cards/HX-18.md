# HX-18: DD_SKIP: the lead's join waits only for MI50s that have a pick
Status: kept (part of build 11) · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: in the fork's release commit. Flags: `DS4_HELPER_DD_SKIP=1` (with DD; default off).

## What and why
- HX-17 showed the lead waits for every helper in every layer. Skip the wait for helpers that were given no expert in that layer.
- Target slice: the per-layer wait for empty helpers: at B1 on tp4h4, 129 of 156 helper waits per token have no pick (MEASURED).

## Prediction
- From the helper-overhead note: most of HX-17's missing gain should come back at B1; less at B8, where more helpers have picks.
- Pre-registered: yes (design note section written before the run).

## Correctness
- All 18 stdouts (3 arms × 3 reps × 2 topologies) byte-identical to the AB reference (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `high` (CP-03).
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- As HX-17: 3 reps, interleaved, fresh process per arm.

## Verdict
- B8 improved (−5.6 %); B1 did not: one of three tp4h4 B1 reps was 19.6 ms, and the best rep (15.2) is only 0.27 ms under DD. Skipping 83 % of waits at B1 returned little time, so the remaining B1 cost is not the empty-helper waits (INFERRED). Kept for B8 and because it is exact.

## Reproduce
- As HX-17 with `DS4_HELPER_DD_SKIP=1`; phases skbt sksw. About 40 GPU-min.
