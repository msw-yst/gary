# PF-09: Prefill discriminator: is XTX #4 the slow member?
Status: finding (cause open) · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: in the fork's release commit. Flags: none.

## What and why
- tp4 prefill was slower than tp3 (TP-05). Run tp3 with XTX #4 swapped in for XTX #3, and tp4h4, and profile every stage.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- Not applicable.

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `high` (CP-03).
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- 2,048-token prompt, 2 reps per arm, untraced, `DS4_PROF` stage times.

## Verdict
- XTX #4 costs ~38 % of 2K prefill when it is in the group; the other members wait for it in the all-reduce stages. Whether that is its x8 link or its position behind a different root complex is not separated yet.
