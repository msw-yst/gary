# CPU pool: sleeping between layers (2026-11-21, data extract)

Sanitised extract behind card NX-13 and [ANATOMY.md](../ANATOMY.md) section 7. Layouts: S and SM as in
[2026-11-21-decode-ab.md](2026-11-21-decode-ab.md) (31 CPU-pool workers), and S2 = two one-card servers side by side (XTX A +
MI50 #1, #2 and XTX B + MI50 #3, #4, 31 workers each) at 4 + 4 users. One server at a time; peaks XTX 71 C, MI50 74 C.

## What the pool does with spin waits (the old default)

All 31 workers spin at 100 % from the first token at every load (the server uses 34.9 busy cores at 1 user and at 8), yet they
are idle 77-87 % of the time at 1 user and 56-60 % at 8. A layer is two short phases (gate/up rows, then down rows, 1-6 us
apart; phase p50 43 us at 1 user, 260 us at 8), and the gap to the next layer is ~300 us (1 user) to ~850 us (8 users) while the
GPU runs the dense part. Spin wake: the last worker starts 0.6 us after the publish.

## Screening

| Mode | Cores | Speed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `futex` (spin 50 us, then futex wait) | 9-13 | -1.5 % (1 user) to -3 % (4-8 users) | one FUTEX_WAKE of ~25 sleepers: last worker starts 27 us p50 / 41-146 us p90 after the publish |
| `nap` (timed sleep to the min of the last 8 gaps) | 15-18 | about even | 8-21 sleepers still at each publish: gaps differ by layer type |
| wake fan-out tree (`STRATA_POOL_WAKE_FANOUT=2`) | - | worse than one wake-all (p90 35 vs 22 us) | not recommended |
| **`pred`** | see below | no slower | last worker start p50 0.6 / p90 1.0 us; 0.4-1 sleepers at publish |
