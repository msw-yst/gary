# DR-04: Drafting inside batched decode steps, with an adaptive rule
Status: kept · Date: 2026-11-08
Code: in Gary's release commit. Flags: `DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX` (default 2).

## What and why
- Run the drafter inside each sequence of a batch step, verify with one multi-row attention call per sequence, and stop drafting when the batch is larger than DSPARK_MAX.

## Prediction
- Rows phase first; the verify row had to come in under 3.5 ms.
- Pre-registered: yes (design note).

## Correctness
- Batched sequences identical to solo (REPORTED).

## Timing
- Hardware: New layout: 3 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3, Gen4 x16) + 3 × MI50 32 GB helpers (MI50 #1–3, Gen4 x4 each); every routed expert on a GPU. XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Reported from notes at the time; the raw runs are not in the published extract.
