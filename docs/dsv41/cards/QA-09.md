# QA-09: Long context: 240K-token retrieval at three depths, the native 1M context, and memory per token
Status: finding (the fuller long-context check is pending) · Date: 2026-11-11
Code: in the fork's release commit (build 11 as deployed, served at `--max-context 262144`). Flags: build 11's (SV-06), drafter on (confidence 0.3, block 3).

## What and why
- Agent traffic reached ~115,000 tokens of context against a 131,072 limit (SV-08), so a larger context was wanted. Nobody had tested this 2.25 bpw pack beyond 128K, and the 256K preset had been marked test-only for that reason, not for a technical one.
- This card records a basic retrieval check at a 262,144-token context, what the model and engine allow, and what each token of context costs in memory.

## Prediction
- Retrieval: no prediction recorded. Pre-registered: no.
- Memory: the per-token formula below was written before the 256K server was started, and predicted "caches" 0.24 GB per XTX and about 43 fewer VRAM experts per card.

## Correctness
- **Retrieval.** A passphrase was placed at 10 %, 50 % and 90 % depth of a ~240,000-token prompt of real repository text, and the model asked to return it (greedy, thinking off). All three were returned exactly (MEASURED). A 120,000-token prompt at 50 % depth was also returned exactly.
- This is a basic retrieval check, not a quality score. Teacher-forced perplexity at depth has not been scored (pending, below).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `high` (CP-03).
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes (build 11).
- The XTX thermal stop line was 95 °C for this test (HW-08).

## Verdict
- **At a 262,144-token context the model retrieves exactly at the start, middle and end of a 240K prompt.** The server then took agent traffic at that context.
- **Memory is not what limits context on this box.** The KV cache costs 920 B a token on each XTX, and the engine pays for it by placing fewer experts in VRAM; those move to the MI50s, which have room. At the native 1M the cost would be about 1.2 GB a card, about 5.5 % of the VRAM experts [I]. The limits are quality, which is untested past 256K, and speed.
- **Speed at 240K:** a cold prompt reads at ~1,590 tok/s (about 2.5 minutes), and decode with the drafter stays at 77–80 tok/s. Cached turns are unaffected.
- **Because context changes the expert placement, a 256K server's outputs are not byte-comparable with a 128K server's** (QA-08).
- **Pending:** the fuller check: needles at 32K–512K (and 1M) at three depths each, decode and prefill at depth, and teacher-forced perplexity on one fixed tail after 123K / 254K / 516K (/ 1.03M) tokens of the same document, with a pre-registered pass of perplexity ≤ 1.03 × the <128K reference and top-1 within 1.5 points. It is queued; results will be added here.

## Reproduce
- The retrieval client and its text are private. Any OpenAI-compatible client can run the same check: place a random passphrase at a known depth in a long document and ask for it back.

## Notes / caveats
- The first attempt at the 240K points was stopped by the thermal watcher: XTX #3 reached 92 °C while reading the 245,840-token prompt (HW-08). Its 120K point was also cut off at the client's token cap before the passphrase was complete. The rows of that attempt are kept in `retrieval.txt`, marked "first". With the stop line at 95 °C, the second attempt passed; XTX #3 peaked at 93 °C (REPORTED, from the temperature log).
- The client's `decode_tok_s` field in `retrieval.txt` is computed over a handful of streamed tokens and is not a decode rate; use the engine's lines.
- The decode rates are over 54–58 tokens each, so they are indicative.

## Addendum (2026-11-13): retrieval to 512K, and the decode cost of context from 1K to 512K

- **Retrieval at a 1M-token context setting (11 Nov, build 11):** 11 of 12 passphrases found up to 512K; the twelfth (512K at 90 % depth) was cut off by a thermal stop of the old XTX #3 before it finished, and the 1M tier was lost the same way (REPORTED from the run notes). A long-context perplexity attempt produced no valid number (a scoring-window setting that yields no logits, a thermal stop, and two run chains racing) (REPORTED). XTX #3 has since been re-pasted (HW-10).

- All five pre-registered predictions passed (noise 0.21 ms). **Even 512K of context costs only about 1.2 ms a token in decode, and reserving a large context costs nothing measurable at short prompts**, though it places fewer experts in VRAM (QA-08).
- **Where the growth comes from:** a stage-stamped run attributed the growth from a 64-token to a 2K-token context (+1.119 ms) to context length itself (+1.135 ms), not to the different output trajectory (−0.116) (MEASURED, DR-06 verify-row-cost.txt). The sparse-attention layers 2–39 each grew by about 23 µs a token.
