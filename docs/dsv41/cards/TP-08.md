# TP-08: Is the four-way tensor-parallel split uneven? A dose probe, and a design for uneven cards
Status: dropped (the re-split is not worth building; the run's own verdict was "no data") · Date: 2026-11-13
Code: the re-split was designed, not built, so it is not in Gary's release commit. Flags: `DS4_TP_SHARES` (an existing test knob, in the release commit, used here for the deliberate skew).

## What and why
- The routed experts are split over the four XTX as blocks of 256 columns in a 3/2/2/2 pattern, with the "3" rotating by layer, so in every layer one member does a third of the work (TP-05). Cards also differ (HW-08), and a fifth card may be added. A design for a weighted split for any number of cards was written: a hybrid (even base plus a per-expert remainder by card weight) modelled at about 1.10 × ideal pace, against 1.33 for weighted columns.
- Before building it, a probe measured how much the current split costs: the base split, a placebo re-split, and a deliberate skew (a known extra dose), on decode rows and prompts.

## Prediction
- The tool must see the deliberate skew in decode (+0.38 ms at one row up to about +1.3 ms at four). Dose in the MoE barrier ≥ 0.22 (predicted 0.44); dose in 8K prompt wall ≥ half of the predicted 4.31 %. Stage-1 gate for building the re-split: ≥ 5 % on 2K prompts and ≥ 3 % on decode. Pre-registered: yes, before the run.

## Correctness
- Identity IDENTICAL for the base split (MEASURED). The skewed arms change the placement and are not expected to be identical.

## Timing
- Hardware: pre-incident set (as in PF-12). Build 14 with the served flags, `--no-expert-cache`, 16K context; base / placebo / skew, 2 reps.

## Verdict
- **The four XTX are already close to evenly loaded.** The skew is visible in decode (the probe works), but even a perfect re-split would gain at most about 1.4 % on decode, under the 3 % gate, and nothing on 8K prompts.
- The run's own verdict is "no data", because the 2K prompt reps were 19 % apart. A rerun for clean prompt reps could not lift decode over its gate, so it was not rerun and the re-split was not built. Prompt timing at two reps is too noisy for a 5 % gate; future prompt preregs use at least three.
