# NX-12: Prompt-path variants that were not kept: four expert streams, batched FP16 conversion
Status: dropped · Date: 2026-11-21
Code: expert streams removed again (not in the fork's commits); `STRATA_PF_DQ_BATCH=G` in the commits, off. Flags: `STRATA_PF_DQ_BATCH=4|8|16`.

## What and why
- Four streams for the experts' GEMMs, to fill ~20 % launch gaps during a read.
- One FP16 dequant launch per group of G experts instead of one per expert.

## Prediction
- Expert streams: a few percent faster. Dequant batching: 5-10 % faster (ESTIMATE before the run).
- Pre-registered: not recorded in the published extracts.

## Correctness
- Both exact: expert streams bit-identical; dequant batching 0 of 505 experts differ (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: one RX 7900 XTX with two MI50 helper caches; EPYC 7642, 256 GB DDR4-2666.
- Software: Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes on ROCm 7.2.
- Workload: cold 38-52k-token prompts, alternating arms.

## Results
| arm | effect | label |
|---|---|---|
| four expert streams, old kernel | 1.3 % slower untimed (its -2.8 % under the timing build was an artefact) | MEASURED |
| four expert streams, tuned row | 14 % slower (38.2 -> 43.5 s) | MEASURED |
| dequant batching G 4 / 8 / 16 | TTFT +2.7 / +4.4 / +6.1 % | MEASURED |
| more hardware queues (`GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=16`) | slower | MEASURED |

## Verdict
- Dropped. A group's FP16 weights (9.8 MB per expert) no longer stay in the 96 MB Infinity Cache between dequant and GEMM (INFERRED from the G trend).

## Reproduce
- `STRATA_PF_DQ_BATCH=8` on "Layout 1" in [README.md](../README.md#launch).

## Notes / caveats
- A fused Q4_K dequant-in-GEMM remains open (~8 %, new kernel, new bits).
