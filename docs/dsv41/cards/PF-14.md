# PF-14: The MI50 prompt helper on the native MXFP4 pack, first try: it engages after a load-check fix, but the first token gets slower
Status: dropped (not adopted on native; superseded by PF-18 and PF-19) · Date: 2026-11-17
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only: the native MXFP4 build and the helper's MXFP4 load check). Flags: `DS4_PF_HELPER=<rows>` (as PF-11), with the native build's load-check fix (branch-only).

## What and why
- On the native MXFP4 pack the MI50 prompt helper (PF-11) refused to start: its load-time check looked for two experts of one layer on a helper and found none in the MXFP4 layout ("load check failed"), so prompts streamed every non-XTX expert to the XTXs as if the helper were off (MEASURED: the engine's start-up line in the same night's settings run).
- This run fixes the load check and asks whether the helper then cuts the native first token, as it did on the 2.25-bit pack (PF-11, PF-12).

## Prediction
- Pre-registered in the run's plan (20 predictions). 10 of 20 came out OUT; the first-token predictions are among them.

## Correctness
- Load check: 4 of 4 helpers covered, worst relative L2 error 0.0017 against a tolerance of 0.03 (MEASURED).
- Teacher-forced, helper off: the fixed build is IDENTICAL to the native build it came from (MEASURED).
- Teacher-forced, helper on: not bit-identical, by design (PF-11). It changes 2.5 % of the top-1 picks, and the KL from the hosted model is unchanged within noise: 0.00558 against 0.00585 (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 17 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 4) and 4 × MI50 32 GB helpers at 135 W. Native MXFP4 pack, served by the internal native build. Arms: base, helper alone (F), and helper plus the short-prompt window path (FW, cutoff 2,048; PF-17).

## Verdict
- **Not adopted.** The helper engages and is quality-neutral on this test, but on native it makes the first token slower.
- The reason given at the time: the chunked prompt pass still streams the 5,552 experts that only the CPU lane holds, so the helper cannot win while experts live on the CPU (INFERRED). PF-18 measured the real cause a day later: what the helper leaves to stream is the cold, disk-backed part of the set, in small batches; PF-19 fixed it.
