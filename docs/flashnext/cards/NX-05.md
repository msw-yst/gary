# NX-05: The MI50s hold every expert the XTX cards do not (tp2 + 4 helpers)
Status: kept · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: in the fork's `flashnext-amd` commits. Flags: `--tensor-parallel 2` with `STRATA_SET="expert-cache-device1=auto expert-cache-device2=auto expert-cache-device3=auto expert-cache-device4=auto"`; the helper caches are the GPUs after the two XTX (CUDA2-5).

## What and why
- The CPU expert pass was memory-bandwidth-bound (~2.1 GB per 4-row window in ~20 ms) and thread count made no difference (NX-03's data). With tp2 the XTX cards hold 63 % of the experts; four MI50s have room for the rest.
- The helper caches are filled striped over the four MI50s, after the XTX caches, so the CPU path is empty.

## Prediction
- No numeric prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- Exact: solo = one card; batched = solo (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 2 × RX 7900 XTX 24 GB, 4 × Instinct MI50 16 GB (135 W cap), EPYC 7642 (48 cores), 256 GB DDR4-2666.
- Software: Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes on ROCm 7.2 (the runtime in Strata's ROCm image). Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Workload: 8 prompts × 384 tokens, temperature 0, presence 0; UD-IQ4_XS.
