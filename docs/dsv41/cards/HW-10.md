# HW-10: 12 Nov hardware work: a fifth MI50 for benches, XTX #3 re-pasted, and a riser at x8 (measured: no cost)
Status: hardware event · Date: 2026-11-12
Code: not applicable. Flags: host tooling.

## What and why
- On 12 Nov the box was powered down to add a fifth MI50 as a bench card, to re-paste XTX #3 (the card that ran 10–20 °C hotter than the others, HW-08) and to fit a network card. Three things followed and are recorded here: the new card's bring-up, XTX #3's thermals, and a PCIe link that came back at half width.

## Prediction
- XTX #3 after re-pasting: a junction-minus-edge gap like the others (≤ ~22 °C).
- A Gen4 x8 link instead of x16 on one XTX: decode under 1 % slower, prompts "possibly several %" (a written estimate before the probe).

## Verdict
- **XTX #3's thermal problem was its paste:** after re-pasting it runs as cool as the others. It is no longer the box's thermal limit on long prompts.
- **x8 instead of x16 on one XTX made no measurable difference** to decode or ~8K prompts (≤ 0.04 ms a token, ≤ 0.2 s, both directions). The prompt estimate was wrong. So the absolute numbers measured between 16:40 and 21:10 stand.
- **The bench MI50 is useful but must not share a thermal watcher blindly:** under sustained benches it reached 78–81 °C and the main queue's watcher (which covers every MI50, correctly) stopped two main-queue jobs on 13 Nov. With its own fan curve it peaks at 66–69 °C.
- Host-side link speeds are now read at the root port; the first post-install check read the card side of the MI50 #5's own bridge and reported x16 wrongly.
