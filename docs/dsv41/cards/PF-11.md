# PF-11: MI50 prompt helper (`DS4_PF_HELPER`): faster short prompts, NOT bit-identical
Status: served (on in the deployed environment from build 13s), validation pending · Date: 2026-11-11 (addendum 2026-11-13; addendum 2026-11-19)
Code: in Gary's release commit. Flags: `DS4_PF_HELPER=<rows>` (default unset = off; tested at 8192), `DS4_PF_HELPER_MIX` (default on with the flag), `DS4_PF_HELPER_CHUNK_MIN=<n>` (default 128 with the flag), `DS4_PF_HELPER_TIME=1` (timing only).

> **NOT bit-identical. Validation in progress; do not treat the numbers below as proof of no effect.**
> This is the first switch in these cards that changes the arithmetic of a default-path computation. Everything on this
> card about quality is a first look, at one depth, on one text, without a higher-precision reference.

## If you turn it on, this is what happens
- **Prompt chunks' experts that the MI50s hold run on the MI50s.** Without the flag, every prompt chunk streams the host-RAM copy of those experts to each XTX (21 GB a member a chunk; PF-10). With it, each MI50 pulls only its tokens' rows, computes its picks and writes back weighted sums, which the members add before the MoE all-reduce. With `_MIX` (the default), experts the MI50s do not hold (moved out of VRAM by the expert cache) still stream as before.
- **Numerically, those experts change kernels and summation order.** On the XTX they run as F16 matrix-core (WMMA) tiles with F32 accumulation, the down projection summed over four tensor-parallel slices. On the MI50 they run with the activations rounded to int8 per 32 values (x) and the hidden activation rounded to int8 (h), as one sum, and the helpers' partial sums are added in a different order. So prompt logits change slightly, and from there the decoded text can change.
- **With `_CHUNK_MIN` (default 128), prompts of 128-511 new tokens also take the chunked path, and so the helpers.** Prompts under 128 tokens are unaffected.
- **Decode is never affected.** The flag acts on prompt chunks only. **With the flag unset, output is byte-identical** to build 11 (MEASURED, below).

## Prediction
- Pre-registered in the run scripts: a fixed cost per prompt of 0.2-0.5 s and a slope of 0.43-0.60 ms a token; a 2,048-token suffix under 2.0 s; a long-context gate on top-1 agreement, KL and perplexity.
- The fixed-cost and 2,048-token predictions were disproved (below). The quality gate passed, narrowly at first.

## Correctness
- **Flag unset:** teacher-forced score IDENTICAL to build 11, in both runs (MEASURED). The added kernels stay inside their code objects' 2 MiB pieces, so the XTX expert placement is unchanged (QA-08's check on the build's code-object sizes, INFERRED).
- **Flag set, decode only (no prompt chunk):** decode IDENTICAL to unset (MEASURED).
- **Flag set, prompt chunks:** not identical, by design. A load-time check compares the MI50 path with a reference on 8 cases on each MI50: worst relative L2 error 0.021, tolerance 0.03 (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `high` (CP-03).
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus Gary's changes (build 11 plus the flag).
- Sweep: a server with a ~16K-token conversation prefix pinned in the cache (14,577 tokens reused on every request), then new suffixes of the sizes below; median of the reps; times from the engine's own per-request lines. Two runs.

- The win is the removed stream: off's ~3 s floor per chunk is the host-RAM expert stream (PF-10), and on removes it. The MI50 side then costs more per token than the stream it replaces at large chunks (slope 0.58 against 0.40 ms a token), so the gain shrinks with chunk size and is gone at 16K. In an earlier run a cold 234K-token prompt was 2.6 % slower with the flag (149.1 against 152.9 s; REPORTED from the internal report).
- The fixed-cost prediction (0.2-0.5 s) was disproved: about 1 s. The 2K-suffix line (< 2.0 s) was disproved: 2.11 s. Most of the remaining fixed cost is the two tensor-parallel all-reduces per layer (INFERRED from the stage profile in the extract).
- Whether the MI50 side is bound by its kernels or by the x4 links is not yet split. The timer flag (`_TIME`) was built for that; its breakdown is pending.

- **What the long2 numbers say:** on this text at this depth, the flag's distance from off is of the same order as moving the chunk boundaries with the flag unset, and perplexity and top-1 accuracy did not get worse. On one measure (median KL against the floor) it is measurably further away, by a small amount.
- **What they cannot show:**
  - **one depth** (~115-124K tokens) and **one text**. Short prompts, where the flag is fastest, have only an earlier 1,000-position short gate (top-1 agreement 96.00 %, against that set's own floor of 96.28 %; REPORTED from the internal report);
  - **no higher-precision reference**: every comparison is between two runs of the same 2.25 bpw pack, so a change that is small next to the pack's own error cannot be told from one that is large next to the full-precision model;
  - nothing about task outcomes, decoded text or the drafter;
  - the earlier gate passed by one position, so it is not evidence either way.

## Validation plan (pending)
- **A Q8 reference.** Compare off and on against a higher-precision pack, and use the pack's own error against Q8 (never measured; QA-04) as the scale for "small".
- **Equivalence margins written down before the run**, on perplexity, top-1 and KL against that reference.
- **A run sized for those margins**: enough positions and documents for the intervals to fit inside them.
- **Controls in the same run:** positive controls (deliberate degradations of known size that the test must catch) and negative controls (off against off, other chunk boundaries).
- **Several depths up to 240K**, plus short prompts (128-8K new tokens), where the flag acts most.
- **Agent and coding task outcomes**, on the same suite as QA-05.
- **Greedy divergence point:** where greedy decoded text first differs from off, over many prompts.
- **Drafter acceptance:** whether the changed prompt state moves it.
- Until that is done the flag stays opt-in and off in build 12.

## Verdict
- **Fast where agent traffic lives:** a 1K-token suffix 3.42 → 1.33 s, 2K 3.71 → 2.11 s, 4K 4.72 → 3.75 s, 8K 6.70 → 5.71 s; equal at 16K. Most prompts under agent traffic add 700-4,096 new tokens (PF-10) [I].
- **Not exact, and not yet validated.** Opt-in, off by default, not part of build 12. The quality evidence so far shows no harm on one text at one depth; it is not evidence of no effect.

## Reproduce
- No published run: the code is in the release commit; a scripted public re-run has not been prepared for this card.

## Notes / caveats
- The sweep's 256 and 512 targets read 222-281 and 415-480 tokens. Prompts under 512 tokens take the 16-token window path unless `_CHUNK_MIN` lowers the chunked-path threshold.
- The 64-token target is unaffected (0.34 s both ways), as designed.
- The timer flag cost nothing measurable (second run, within 0.1 s at every size).

## Addendum (2026-11-13): served, the validation run, batching, and what came next

> **Still NOT bit-identical, and the validation is still not complete.** What changed is that the flag is now on in the deployed environment.

- **Status.** From build 13s the served environment sets `DS4_PF_HELPER=8192` (SV-10), for its time-to-first-token gain: in the served benchmark it cut a 2K prompt's TTFT from 4.3–4.4 s to 2.5–3.0 s in the same run (MEASURED, SV-11). This was a deployment decision taken with the validation below still inconclusive.
- **Port to build 12.** On build 12 with the flag: 1K 1.21 s, 2K 2.11 s, 4K 3.53 s, 8K 5.94 s, within ±10 % of the first port (REPORTED). Two variants were dropped: 4,096 rows instead of 8,192 (worse at 4K and 8K) and F16 operands on the MI50 (`DS4_PF_PREC`: accuracy held, but 1.73–2.52 × slower on the bench) (REPORTED).
- **The validation run (11–12 Nov) is formally inconclusive.** Its pre-registered verdict rests on a Q8 reference, and only 4 of the 20 required Q8 cells could be produced: a Q8 pack does not fit in host RAM beside everything else (QA-04). Its final line reads "inconclusive: the harness failed a control", estimate +0.00138 nats [−0.00010, +0.00295] against the partial Q8 reference. Against the run's other (high-precision) reference arm the difference was −7.5e-5 [−3.9e-4, +2.1e-4], which that arm's rule calls "no effect that matters" (REPORTED from the run's summary, as read in the ledger).
- **Two defects in the validation harness** were found by a review of its statistics: the placebo check could print "calibrated" for uncalibrated data, and bootstrap blocks of 25 positions gave intervals about 1.5 × too narrow (REPORTED). **The long2 intervals in the quality table above used blocks of 25, so they are probably too narrow by about that factor** [I]. A later reanalysis on 49,152 positions put on-vs-floor at −1.1e-4 ± 1.4e-4 (REPORTED).
- **Batched output.** With the flag on, a prompt's sums depend on how it is chunked, so under concurrency batched greedy output can differ from solo greedy output (5 of 8 identical); with it off, 8 of 8 (MEASURED, SV-10).
- **Text-level comparison does not work as a ruler.** On 12 prompts (one user, expert cache on), greedy output with the flag on matched off bit for bit on 1 of 12 and diverged on 10, after a median of 12 words. With sampling, even an exact repeat of the same server and seeds diverged within ~3–5 words (23 of 24 pairs different), so text similarity cannot separate the flag from ordinary sampling (REPORTED from the run's own table). Only task-level scoring can say whether the change matters (QA-05).
- **On the critical path.** The engine's own counters on build 12pf: members wait for their helpers for 17–22 % of prompt wall time (REPORTED). A one-pass tiled kernel for the helper's prompt sets, `DS4_PF_TILE`, took a further 15 % off TTFT at every length, bit-exact against this flag's path (PF-12).
- **Long prompts.** In the served benchmark, the flag-off arm in a later session read 8K in 5.9 s and 32K in 18.9 s, against 6.9 s and 25.9 s with the flag on earlier; across sessions the flag looks slower from 8K up (MEASURED / INFERRED, SV-11). The like-for-like comparison comes with the build-15 benchmark (SV-14).

## Addendum (2026-11-19): the helper on the native MXFP4 pack
- The helper now also runs on the native pack (internal build, not in a release). It needed an MXFP4 load check and room kept free on each MI50 for its buffers (PF-14, PF-18).
- On native it is a win only together with a RAM store that leaves out the helper-held experts and a stream that keeps its copy batches full (PF-19), and DMA straight from the store (PF-20): a 2K prompt's first token 15.2 → 3.3 s, 8K 16.5 → 7.3 s, 14K 26.3 → 12.6 s (MEASURED).
- **Still not bit-identical.** On native the load check's worst relative L2 error is 0.0017 (tolerance 0.03); teacher-forced, the helper changes 2.5 % of top-1 picks and leaves the KL from the hosted model unchanged within noise (0.00558 against 0.00585) (MEASURED, PF-14). Text closeness to the baseline passes in every native run. The validation plan above is still open.
