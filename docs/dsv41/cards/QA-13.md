# QA-13: Where the mixed pack's remaining quality gap comes from: the non-expert tensors matter, and expert choice matters more than first thought
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-18
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch: mixed per-expert precision, QA-12). Measurement: teacher-forced KL against the hosted model's log-probabilities.

## What and why
- QA-12's x25c pack (native MXFP4 for the 2,960 hottest experts on top of the 2.25-bit pack) reached KL 0.0210 from the hosted model, against a bar of 0.019 and native's 0.0054. Two questions decide the next pack: how much of the residual is the non-expert tensors (attention, shared expert, head: 5- and 8-bit in the 2.25-bit pack, BF16 in native), and how much better a perfect choice of the same number of experts could do.

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: the non-expert term b = 0.000–0.002 (central), within −0.002…+0.003; an oracle choice of 2,960 experts KL 0.012–0.014.

## Correctness
- Teacher-forced, deterministic within a binary (QA-12's gate). Repeating QA-12's x25c cell on the same binary and pack was **not** bitwise: KL between the two runs 2.8e-3 mean, top-1 0.981. The likely cause is placement differing between server starts (ME-13); the run allows about ±0.001 of placement noise on a KL difference (MEASURED; the cause INFERRED).

## Timing
- 18 Nov, the test machine; 12 prompts with high-precision references, 2,900 scored positions. Arms: x25c again (H), an oracle pack with the 2,960 experts the evaluation itself picks most (O), x25c with the non-expert tensors from the native pack (R).

- Non-expert term, measured directly (H − R): 0.00361, against 0.000–0.002 predicted (MEASURED). A three-point fit gives 0.00235 (MEASURED, script fit).
- The native share at the scored positions: x10 0.467, x25c 0.634, oracle 0.722 (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **The non-expert tensors matter:** native non-expert tensors alone take x25c from 0.0206 to 0.0170. Making them native costs about 3.14 GB more on the lead (REPORTED from the plan). A pack aiming at ≤ 0.01 needs them.
- **Choice matters too:** even an oracle choice at x25c's size leaves 0.0165, three times native's KL and above its predicted 0.012–0.014. QA-12's reading that the share matters and the choice barely does (INFERRED there, from two packs and a re-ranking) holds for re-ranking within a pack, not for this oracle bound (INFERRED).
- Expected KL for the five-helper packs (predicted 0.0066–0.0071) may be optimistic; those runs are queued behind the fifth MI50's qualification (HW-14).

## Reproduce
- Teacher-forced scoring against a hosted model's log-probabilities on the same prompts, swapping the pack's non-expert tensors and the expert set one at a time.

## Notes / caveats
- Mean KL over 12 prompts; no task outcomes.
- Two earlier gate runs that night stopped correctly: one because the no-manifest build did not equal the native build at unequal placement (KL 3.3e-3, top-1 0.974), and a served-speed run of larger mixed packs gave NO DATA when its equal-placement search could not reproduce the target placement. No mixed result is trusted on a build that is not identical to its base.
