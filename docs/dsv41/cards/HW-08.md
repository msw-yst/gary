# HW-08: Four XTX boards, four sets of limits; one hot card trips the watcher on long-prompt prefill
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: —. Flags: —.

## What and why
- The four RX 7900 XTX cards are different boards from three vendors. Under a sustained agent workload (SV-08), XTX #4 looked slower than the others and XTX #3 ran much hotter.
- This card records each board's limits and what they mean for tensor-parallel timing and thermal headroom.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the readings.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- Not applicable.

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV.
- The power caps are set to 90 % of each board's own default limit, not to one common wattage.

## Verdict
- **XTX #4 is the slowest card because of its board's limits.** It has the lowest default power limit (291 W) and the lowest top clock point (2870 MHz), so a 90 % cap leaves it 2–3 % below the others in live clock. In tensor parallel every member waits for the slowest at each all-reduce. In the HX-20 timeline the attention all-reduce's last arrival is usually the same member, by 1–5 µs a layer, about 0.1 ms a token [I: the device-to-card mapping is not confirmed].
- **XTX #3 is a thermal-interface problem, not a load problem.** It draws the same power as the others but runs 10–20 °C hotter. It has not had its thermal paste replaced, unlike the other three. On a long prompt (a 245,840-token prefill, which is burstier than decode) it reached 92 °C. The thermal watcher stopped the server, as designed.
- The response was to raise the XTX stop line to 95 °C for long-context tests, keep 80 °C for the MI50s, and repaste or replace the card. Equalising XTX #4's cap is deferred until larger slices are fixed.

## Reproduce
- Read `power1_cap`, `power1_cap_default`, `pp_od_clk_voltage` and `pp_dpm_sclk` under `/sys/class/drm/card*/device/` for each card.

## Notes / caveats
- The readings were hand-transcribed from one read-only session.
- The temperatures are from 5-second status samples during the agent run. The 92 °C peak is from the watcher's own log.

## Addendum (2026-11-13)
- **XTX #3 was re-pasted on 12 Nov.** Under a ~270 W load it then peaked at 69 °C junction, 20 °C above its edge sensor, in line with the others (REPORTED, HW-10). The thermal-interface reading above is confirmed.
- **XTX #2 was replaced on 13 Nov** after a third SMU hang that ended in a host power loss (HW-09). Its replacement is a different vendor's board, freshly re-pasted, with a junction-minus-edge gap of about 28 °C under a burn test.
- **XTX #4** now runs its board's second firmware position, whose default power limit is 339 W instead of 291 W; it is capped at 305 W (REPORTED, HW-09). The table above describes the boards as of 10 Nov.
