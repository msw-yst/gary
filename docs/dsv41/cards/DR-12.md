# DR-12: Drafter block 6 instead of 3 on the native pack: bit-identical, one user 11–12 % faster; held out of release 4 by its four-user no-harm bar
Status: finding (NO-GO for release 4 by its pre-registered bar; the selective form's run is incomplete, DR-14) · Date: 2026-11-19 (addendum 2026-11-20)
Code: no new code for block 6 itself (a drafter setting, DR-01); measured on the internal native build. A selective form (block 6 only at low concurrency) is on an internal branch, not yet run.

## What and why
- DR-01 set the drafter's block at 3. DR-11 showed the drafter offers full drafts 90 % of the time on the native pack, so on native a longer block might land more tokens a round.

## Prediction
- Pre-registered for the decision run: one-user decode not worse (upper 95 % CI ≤ 1.02); four users no harm (lower 95 % CI of block 6 / block 3 > 0.98).

## Correctness
- Placement frozen and equal in both arms; bits IDENTICAL, short and long prompts, two repeats, same token counts (first run) and again in the decision run (MEASURED).
- The first run's verdict was INCOMPLETE: its "computed" check counted 8 of 6 and 12 of 6 requests. The count, not the data, was wrong (a parser counted extra prompt lines written by the server's own follow-up calls); re-scored from the run's logs: 6 of 6, VALID (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 19 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 4) + 4 × MI50 helpers; native MXFP4 pack, internal native build, frozen placement. First run: 2 and 3 users. Decision run: eight served arms F3 F6 F6 F3 × 2; one user at 2K and 8K (sampled), 2 and 4 users at 2K.

## Verdict
- **One user: 11–12 % faster, outputs identical, both CIs clear of 1.** Block 6 lands more tokens a round on native.
- **NO-GO for release 4 by its own bar:** at four users the lower CI was 0.969, not above 0.98, so the run could not rule out a 2 % loss.
- That four-user cell could not have been affected: at four users no step drafts (`DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX=3`, DR-10), so the true ratio there is 1.000 (stated in the run's own summary). Its batched window was also only about 9 s of an 83 s cell, with s_d 2.77 % (REPORTED, read from the run at the time). The NO-GO is the bar working on an under-powered cell, not evidence of harm.
- The first run's two-user +21 % (n = 2) was noise: the decision run measured × 1.015.
- Next: block 6 only when few users are active, with a longer batched window and n sized for it (built, queued).

## Reproduce
- Serve the native pack with the drafter's block at 3 and at 6, frozen placement, and compare one-user ms a token over interleaved arms.

## Notes / caveats
- The pre-registered bar was corrected to "no harm" at four users before any run, because four users never draft. Keeping the bar after learning that the cell could not move was the rule; the follow-up fixes the cell instead.
