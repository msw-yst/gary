# HW-15: What limits P2P on this board: the receiving GPU's power state, not the board, the BIOS or the I/O die
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-18
Code: no engine change. Lab probes (latency, landing, bandwidth, feed) that are not in this release.

## What and why
- Direct GPU-to-GPU writes lost to the host route for the decode all-reduce (AR-10, and the P2P write-up). Before redesigning, a set of lab probes asked whether the limit was the board (PCIe switch, root complexes, I/O die), the firmware settings, or the GPUs, and whether the MI50s' x4 links need a PCIe switch.

## Prediction
- None written as numbers; each probe had a question.

## Correctness
- Lab probes only; every transfer was checked, 0 wrong values in every probe (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 18 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX and 4 × MI50, idle apart from the probe unless stated; one probe in a served run (the link and clock monitor).

## Verdict
- **The P2P pair cap and about half the hand-off latency are the receiving GPU's power state** (its fabric and SoC clocks at idle), set by the driver, **not the board, the I/O die or the BIOS.** The platform's own share of a hand-off is 0.2–0.5 µs, and a PCIe switch would remove at most 0.1–0.4 µs.
- **The host route wins the decode all-reduce by structure:** a write lands in host DRAM about 1 µs sooner than in a peer's VRAM, on the receiving card's inbound path (INFERRED).
- **No MI50 PCIe switch for the current design:** the MI50 links are nearly idle while serving.
- Today's ~12 GB/s prompt staging was a software limit, not board bandwidth: the host can deliver 56 GB/s to four XTX (INFERRED; PF-15 to PF-20 then removed most of the staging).
- Keeping the XTX clocks up raises P2P bandwidth and halves the hand-off in the lab, but did not help served runs and costs idle power (CP-06). XTX clocks are already at full while serving.

## Reproduce
- No published probe. The method: time a flag hand-off and bulk writes between pairs with the receiver idle and with its clocks forced up; read the link and clock levels from sysfs while serving.

## Notes / caveats
- One pull-bandwidth reading (3.6–5 GB/s a pair, about 9 two days earlier) is unexplained.
- An earlier per-step cost in one probe (about 8 µs) was an artefact of that probe and is withdrawn.

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output) or INFERRED (our own reading).
