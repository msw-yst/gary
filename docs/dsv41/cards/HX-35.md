# HX-35: 4-bit (MXFP4) expert kernels: eight variants and a flat-grid MI50 kernel, all bit-exact; the XTX is already at bandwidth, the MI50 gains 20–34 %
Status: finding (the MI50 flat kernel carried into HX-36; the XTX variants not needed) · Date: 2026-11-17 (MI50 rounds 2026-11-18)
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branches). Flags: `DS4_MXK` (variant choice), `DS4_MXW` (the MI50 flat kernel, 1 / 3 / 4), `DS4_G906_FLAT` (the same walk for the 2-bit MI50 kernels) (branch-only, default off).

## What and why
- Native MXFP4 experts run on the XTXs (their own share) and on the MI50 helpers. A kernel survey estimated the room: per expert call at one row the MI50 ran 62.8 µs against a 23.5 µs floor (2.7×), the XTX 35.5 against 21.0 (1.7×) (REPORTED from the survey). Eight variants were built bit-identical by design and benched on real expert bytes; then three more rounds on the MI50 alone.

## Prediction
- First round: MI50 37–54 µs, XTX 29–40 µs per expert call. Missed on the XTX: it was already at bandwidth on the real shapes.

## Correctness
- All eight variants BIT-EXACT against the current kernels on both cards (MEASURED). One fused variant hangs on the XTX (it needs every block resident) and gives no data there.
- In the engine's own kernels, the MI50 flat walk against off: 30 BITWISE, 0 differ, across the 2-bit window path (batch 1 / 2 / 4) and the MXFP4 window path (`DS4_MXW=1/3/4`) (MEASURED; identity only, no timing).

## Timing
- 17–18 Nov, the test machine: one MI50 (the 18 Nov rounds on MI50 #5, HW-14) and one XTX, single-card benches on real expert bytes. Served effect: HX-36.

## Verdict
- **XTX expert kernels are done:** at bandwidth on the shapes the engine actually runs.
- **The MI50 kernel had 20–34 % to give on served-shape windows**, bit-exact. The first estimate of its decode value was small (0.17–0.36 ms a token, 1–3 %, because MI50 trips are on the critical path only part of the time; INFERRED). Served: HX-36.

## Reproduce
- No published bench for these variants; the release's `tools/qbench/` (HX-33) times the MI50 kernels as they are in the release.

## Notes / caveats
- Isolated-kernel numbers. The first served A/B of the flat kernel could not see the gain under page-cache and sampler noise (HX-36).

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output) or INFERRED (our own reading).
