# DR-08: Drafter policy revisited: sampled drafts, the adaptive rule at its design point, and a depth × confidence sweep
Status: dropped (the depth 5, confidence 0.4 candidate did not confirm; the served policy stays block 3, confidence 0.3); `DS4_DSPARK_SAMPLE` and the adaptive rule dropped · Date: 2026-11-13
Code: in the fork's release commit. Flags: `--dspark-block`, `--dspark-conf` (existing); `DS4_DSPARK_SAMPLE=1`, `DS4_DSPARK_ADAPT=1` (default off).

## What and why
- DR-06's cost model said the served policy (block 3, confidence 0.3) was near the optimum on live traffic, but the settings came from short greedy tests (DR-01). Three things were tried on served, sampled traffic:
  - `DS4_DSPARK_SAMPLE=1`: draw the drafts from the drafter's own distribution instead of taking its argmax, which in theory raises acceptance under sampled verification;
  - `DS4_DSPARK_ADAPT=1` at its design point (confidence 0.1, block 5), after SV-09's −11 % headline for it turned out to be an artefact of output length;
  - a sweep of block 2–5 × confidence 0.3 / 0.4 against today's (3, 0.3), with a placebo (same policy, other seeds).

## Prediction
- SAMPLE: −8 to +3 % ms a generated token. ADAPT (0.1, 5): −3 to +1.5 %, dropped if it yields no more tokens a round than the base. Pre-registered: yes (REPORTED from the run notes).
- Sweep: every cell within about ±1.5 % of the base; the best cell +0.5 % (−0.8 to +1.8); (5, 0.4) −0.1 % [−1.5, +1.5]. Pre-registered: yes, in the sweep's prereg, before the run.
- Confirm run: the same per-cell predictions ((5, 0.4) −0.1 % [−1.5, +1.5]; (3, 0.4) −0.4 % [−1.5, +0.5]; (5, 0.3) +0.5 % [−0.8, +1.8]); a cell counts only if its change exceeds the placebo noise. Pre-registered: yes.

## Correctness
- Policy changes alter only how many drafts are verified, never the keep rule, so output is exact in distribution. SAMPLE uses the speculative-sampling rule with the drafter's distribution as q, also exact in distribution. No separate identity gate (sampled runs).

## Timing
- SAMPLE and ADAPT: build 12, production phase (served mix, sampled), 11 Nov.
- Sweep: build 12pf, 8 drafter prompts, sampled at temperature 0.6, 2 reps, 13 Nov 19:02, on the changed XTX set (HW-09); 65 of 72 request positions matched in each rep.
- Confirm run: build 15, 13 Nov 23:58, the same harness, 4 reps, blocks 3 and 5 only (by design), confidence 0.3 and 0.4, placebo = the base with other seeds; 62–66 of 72 request positions matched a rep.

## Verdict
- **SAMPLE: dropped.** 13 % slower: on this traffic the drafter's own distribution is accepted less often than its argmax.
- **ADAPT: dropped.** At its design point it verifies more rows for fewer tokens a round.
- **Sweep: (5, 0.4) was an adopt-candidate.** It beat the base by 3.97 % against a 2.44 % placebo spread on two reps, about 1.6 times the noise. Raising the confidence floor to 0.4 trims wasted rows (at depth 5: 2.57 → 1.79 rows a round) while keeping the depth for long accepted runs [I].
- **Confirm run: NOT CONFIRMED.** On build 15 with 4 reps, (5, 0.4) came in at −1.58 % against a sampled-content noise of 3–4 %, and no cell was separable from the base. The served policy stays at block 3, confidence 0.3. The run's own summary printed "no data (3 of 7 cells)"; that is an artefact of running only blocks 3 and 5 by design, so the four block-2 and block-4 cells were empty.
- Resolving 1–2 % here would need many more reps, or greedy requests [I].
- The prediction (everything within ±1.5 %) was wrong in direction for most cells, and the placebo moved −2.4 % on its own: this harness cannot resolve 2 % on two reps.
