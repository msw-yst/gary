# FG-36: A survey of XTX speed ideas against our code: most are already in, four are dead, seven are open
Status: desk (survey and code reading; nothing built or run) · Date: 2026-11-13
Code: none (desk study, against the build-14 source). Flags: none.

## What and why
- After build 14 we surveyed published RX 7900 XTX (gfx1100) speed-ups for decode matrix-vector kernels, matrix-core prompt kernels and multi-GPU, and checked each against our source: already done, dead, or open with a size estimate.

## Verdict
- **Most published XTX tricks were already in the engine; the open items were all estimates.** The ranking set part of the next queue.
- What happened to some of the open items: the four-way split was measured and closed (TP-08: a perfect re-split ≤ 1.4 % decode); a different down-kernel change (more tiles a block) won on the bench and gave nothing in the engine (FG-13); moving the drafter round's host path onto the GPU was later sized below what an A/B could resolve and shelved (DR-19). Batched multi-prompt prefill and an int8 prompt all-reduce were not built.

## Notes / caveats

Numbers are labelled MEASURED (read from a raw run file or a script-written summary), REPORTED (from a commit message or write-up at the time, not re-checked against raw output) or INFERRED (our own reading).
