# HW-09: XTX #2's power controller hung and the host lost power; the card was replaced
Status: incident (card replaced) · Date: 2026-11-13
Code: not applicable. Flags: host tooling.

## What and why
- XTX #2's system management unit (SMU, the card's power controller) had hung once before (HW-05, 10 Nov) and again on 12 Nov 04:31 between runs, at near-idle; that time the watchdog added after HW-05 saw the driver's message and rebooted the host, as designed (REPORTED from the driver and watchdog logs).
- On 13 Nov at 13:09:07, seconds after a light device check had started, the driver reported XTX #2's SMU "in hanged state", once a second. Between about 13:09:21 and 13:09:25 the host lost AC power. The board management controller then restarted it (its power-restore policy), and POST hung. The box was drained and powered on by hand at 13:32; all nine cards came back without errors (REPORTED from the kernel journal and the management controller's event log).

## Verdict
- **XTX #2 was removed.** Its slot was tried with two other RX 7900 XTX boards under a 6-minute burn test (both cards loaded, fans at 100 %), against the junction-minus-edge gap of at most about 22 °C that the re-pasted XTX #3 met (HW-10):
  - the first replacement showed a ~42 °C gap after re-pasting (mount or contact suspected) and was rejected;
  - the second, freshly re-pasted, showed a ~28 °C gap (junction 74–77 °C, memory 72–74 °C at 277–369 W) and was accepted. **From 13 Nov 14:37 "XTX #2" is this board.**
- **XTX #4** has a two-position firmware switch. During the work it was moved to its second position and back; after moving back it failed to initialise on every boot. It now runs on the second position, whose default power limit is 339 W (291 W before), capped at 305 W (REPORTED).
- Cards after this point run on a changed XTX set, so absolute numbers before and after 13 Nov 13:09 are not directly comparable; within-run A/B ratios are unaffected. Each card's Timing section says which set it used.
- Open: the watchdog's pattern now needs the "hanged state" wording too; the cause of the power loss is unknown.
