# PF-18: The MI50 prompt helper on the native MXFP4 pack works, and is slower: the helpers took the hot RAM-held experts and left the cold, disk-backed tail in small batches
Status: finding (the cause fixed in PF-19) · Date: 2026-11-19
Code: not in the release commit of Gary, our fork of Strata (internal branch only). Flags: `DS4_PF_HELPER=2048` with `DS4_PF_HELPER_ROOM=1` (keeps room on each MI50 for the helper's buffers) and an MXFP4 load check (branch-only).

## What and why
- PF-15 showed that native prompt reads re-send to the XTXs the 7,136 experts the MI50s already hold, because the MI50 prompt helper could not start: the MI50s were full. `DS4_PF_HELPER_ROOM=1` keeps about 0.5 GiB free on each MI50 for the helper's buffers (28 fewer expert slots each at 2,048 rows), and a fixed load check accepts MXFP4.
- Expected: each XTX stages 55 % fewer experts, so a 2K first token × 0.45–0.70.

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: 2K first token × 0.45–0.70 (GO at ≤ 0.75); 8K × 0.97–1.05 (the helper does not take chunks over 2,048 rows); decode × 1.00–1.025; MI50 prompt sets 0.8–3.0 s a device.

## Correctness
- Load check: 12 MXFP4 cases on 4 of 4 helpers, worst relative L2 error 0.0017, tolerance 0.03 (MEASURED).
- The helper is not bit-identical by design (PF-11). Text closeness to the baseline on the helper's own 2K cells: 3 of 3 close (PASS) (MEASURED).
- Within-arm repeats: 1 of 6 identical in both arms. The baseline did not repeat itself either; the cause was placement differing between server starts (ME-13).

## Timing
- 19 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (tensor-parallel 4) and 4 × MI50 32 GB helpers at 135 W; one EPYC Zen 2 16-core host, DDR4 at 2933 (HW-13). Native MXFP4 pack. Arms P1 B1 P2 B2 and the new binary with the baseline environment (U); three 2K and three 8K cells each, greedy, placement frozen.

**Desk analysis of the run (19 Nov):**
| finding | label |
|---|---|
| The experts the helpers hold are the hot ones, which were already in the RAM store. The 5,724 still sent to each XTX are the cold ones, mostly disk-backed: disk bytes a read fell only 42.7 → 37.8 GB (P2 against B2) while the data sent fell 59.6 → 26.9 GB a XTX | MEASURED (counters) / INFERRED (the reading) |
| The host copies the streamed experts in batches of about 21 in parallel; with the helper, every held expert cuts a run, so batches averaged about 2 experts and the copy parallelism collapsed: host staging 18.1 s against 12.8–13.1 s, about 1.5 against 4.6 GB/s a XTX | INFERRED (from the source); the batch size MEASURED in PF-19 (1.9) |
| About 40 GB a read still came from the disk in **both** arms: the stream's read-ahead asks for file pages of experts the store already holds | INFERRED; matches PF-15's counters |

## Verdict
- **The helper works and its MI50 work is fully hidden (about 1 ms of waiting), but the read got slower.** Moving the hot experts' work to the MI50s left the XTXs streaming the cold tail from disk, in small batches. Sending fewer bytes made the read slower because which bytes, and from where, matter more than how many.
- Pre-registered verdict NO DATA: the helper arm drifted between its two runs (P2 / P1 × 0.611; the first arm's first cells ran on a cold page cache). Both helper arms were slower than every baseline cell.
- The fix follows from the diagnosis: keep the helper-held experts out of the RAM store so it holds the streamed ones, and order the stream so batches stay full (PF-19).
