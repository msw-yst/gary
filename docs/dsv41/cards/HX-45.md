# HX-45: Waking the MI50s early on guessed experts: dismissed at the desk as worth about 0, never measured
Status: desk (dismissed without a measurement; the reasoning covers compute, not the wake-up path) · Date: 2026-11-18

## What and why
- A performance plan on 18 Nov listed "start guessed experts early on the MI50s" and dismissed it: the MI50s hold their experts' weights already, and layer L+1's input does not exist until layer L's all-reduce finishes, so there is nothing to start early.

## Verdict
- **The reasoning holds for the expert maths, not for the wake-up.** Part of each helper window is the MI50 noticing the request and reading its input (~21 µs); a pre-wake on the guessed layer could hide some of that, but only in layers where the lead waits and an MI50 is last into the join.
- On 21 Nov the CPU lane, not an MI50, was last in 79 % of waiting layers and the MI50s carried under 10 % of the excess wait (measured in the run of FG-33). So the prize is small, and the item stays dropped (INFERRED).
