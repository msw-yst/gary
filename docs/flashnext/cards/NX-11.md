# NX-11: Concurrent prompt read, and lent experts on the GPU
Status: kept (opt-in; recommended for agent serving on one card) · Date: 2026-11-21
Code: in the fork's `flashnext-amd` commits. Flags: `STRATA_BATCH_CONCURRENT_READ=2`, `STRATA_BATCH_LENT_GPU=1`.

## What and why
- Prompts serialise: decoding slots run only between a prompt's 8,192-token chunks, for half as long as a chunk took. A decoding agent stalled 7.7 s at a time while someone else's 45k prompt was read, and the read took 62 s instead of 45.
- Mode 2 of the concurrent read reads the prompt on its own thread beside the decoding slots, keeping the prompt's loan of 1,556 expert-cache slots (taken before the read, returned after).
- During a read the decoding slots computed the lent experts on the CPU, which rounds differently, so their text depended on timing. `STRATA_BATCH_LENT_GPU=1` sends a lent expert to a VRAM group pointing at its blob's device alias in the pinned host arena: the GPU hit kernel, in the hit launch.

## Prediction
- Shorter read window and no long stalls, at a lower decode rate during the read; lent experts on the GPU exact.
- Pre-registered: not recorded in the published extracts.

## Correctness
- Concurrent read: every prompt read alone gives the baseline's text (MEASURED).
- Lent experts on the GPU: 4 × 12k prompts sent at once == alone 16/16 in four launches (without it 1/4); ~97k lent experts per launch on the GPU, 0 on the CPU. A 2,500-token stream beside a read equals its alone text in 3 of 4 launches; the 4th flips at character 7,601, where the stream alone also flips once in 7 launches (a solo near-tie) (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: one RX 7900 XTX with two MI50 helper caches; EPYC 7642, 256 GB DDR4-2666.
- Software: Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes on ROCm 7.2. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Workload: a thinking-on stream of 2,500 tokens with a 44.7k cold prompt arriving 4 s in; c1 / c2 / c4 cold prompts; alternating A F D N F A.

## Results

| A old baseline | 21.8 | 7.8 s | 62.6 s | 202.6 s | MEASURED |

F's two launches give identical texts in every phase (A's do not) (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- Kept, opt-in: it trades the decoding slots' rate during a read for the new prompt's TTFT and steady streams, which is what agent timeouts need.

## Reproduce
- "Layout 1" in [README.md](../README.md#launch) with the two switches; a decoding stream plus a cold 45k prompt.

## Notes / caveats
- One-card only: tensor parallel has no batched prompt path.
- Four cold prompts at once finish slightly later with F than with N (159.7 vs 152.1 s): the read shares the GPU with decoding.
