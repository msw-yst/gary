# QA-07: Build 10's "DEGENERATE continuations" flag in the batched-vs-solo check
Status: explained · Date: 2026-11-09
Code: in the fork's release commit (check script). Flags: —.

## What and why
- The batched-vs-solo checker printed "continuations DEGENERATE" for some arms.

## Prediction
- No prediction was recorded before the run.
- Pre-registered: no.

## Correctness
- With the expert cache off, every batched sequence (B2, B8) is identical to its solo reference for all 256 tokens, with 0 mix-ups (MEASURED).
- The flag fires when any sequence's distinct-4-gram ratio d4 is below 0.5. The flagged sequences have d4 0.46 / 0.49, and their solo runs have exactly the same d4 (MEASURED).

- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- Not a timing card.
