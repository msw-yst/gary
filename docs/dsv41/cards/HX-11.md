# HX-11: Defer helper experts by one layer (C) and partial deferral
Status: shelved · Date: 2026-11-10
Code: in the fork's release commit (default off). Flags: `DS4_HELPER_DEFER=1`, `DS4_HELPER_DEFER_MAXW=<w>` (default off).

## What and why
- Let the XTX continue to layer L+1 without the helper's layer-L rows and add them one layer late (an approximation). Partial form: defer only picks whose router weight is below a threshold w.

## Prediction
- 12–15 % at B1 (design note); quality expected to need a gate because it is not exact.
- Pre-registered: yes (design note).
- Quality pass: top-1 within 0.5 pp of off and ppl ≤ +1 %.

## Correctness
- Full C, teacher-forced vs off (tp3h3): top-1 agreement 83.1 %, ppl 3.36 → 3.99: GATE FAIL (MEASURED).
- Window-1 and window-16 scores, tp4h4: full C ppl +11.69 % FAIL; w 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.2 PASS; w 0.3 / 0.5 FAIL (MEASURED).

## Timing
- Hardware: 4 × RX 7900 XTX (XTX #1–3 Gen4 x16, XTX #4 Gen4 x8) + 4 × MI50 32 GB at 135 W (Gen4 x4 each). XTX at minimum power cap with −50 mV. MI50 DPM `auto`.
- Software: ROCm 7.14 on Linux; Strata v0.2.9 plus this fork's changes. Kernel version: not captured in the published extracts.
- As HX-10; MAXW sweep 1 rep per w (MEASURED).
