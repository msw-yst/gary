# QA-16: Expert weight formats compared in weight space only (QLADDER step 1), what fits, and the community quants
Status: finding (weight space only; the output-error run that would rank the formats has not run) · Date: 2026-11-20
Code: no engine change. Offline CPU tools: native MXFP4 experts re-quantized to each format with a public quantizer (delphi.cpp, MIT) and compared with the original.

**Read this card as weight-space error only.** Weight error says how far the stored numbers are from native. It does not say how far the expert's output moves on real inputs, which is what quality depends on, and it cannot measure what an importance matrix does (an importance matrix trades plain weight error for output error). The output-space run needs captured expert inputs; the capture in FG-25 wrote 0 bytes and has to be re-run.

## What and why
- Before writing kernels for new expert formats (QA-15), compare the candidates on equal terms: llama.cpp's IQ2 / IQ3 / Q2_K / Q3_K / IQ4_XS, and ik_llama.cpp's IQ2_KS / IQ2_K / IQ3_K, against native MXFP4.
- Sample: layers 2, 20 and 38; cold experts (8 a layer at the 2-bit levels, 4 at the others) and the 4 most-picked a layer; 3 matrices an expert.

- **Hot and cold differ by under 0.1 % for every format**, and the spread across experts is 0.1–1.4 %: weight error carries no per-expert signal, so it cannot choose which experts to keep native (as QA-12's first stage found).
- **At equal size the ik formats have less weight error:** IQ2_KS 8 % less than the IQ2_XXS–IQ2_XS line at 2.19 bits, IQ2_K 10 % less than IQ2_XS–IQ2_S at 2.38, IQ3_K 13 % less than IQ3_XXS–IQ4_XS at 3.44 and 21 % less than Q3_K at the same size (MEASURED / INFERRED interpolation). Q2_K and Q3_K are the worst at their sizes. Gary has no kernel for any ik format on any device.
- **The 2.25-bit pack was made with an importance matrix** (its metadata records 585 entries over 1,000 chunks; the calibration data is not recorded) by ISTA-DASLab's GSQ-RCO method. Its IQ2_XXS weights carry 10 % more weight error than a plain re-quantization from native (0.136 against 0.124). That is expected of an importance matrix, and it was made from BF16, not from MXFP4; only the output-space run can say whether it is better where it counts (MEASURED / INFERRED).
- A separate measurement with the same tools on a different model (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, in output space) found the importance matrix's effect large at 3 bits and below, and found that two published quant files did not store their routed experts at the format their names give, in the layers sampled (REPORTED; not our model, not re-checked here).

## What fits (from STACK's placement log; MEASURED sizes, INFERRED sums)
- A whole-model IQ3_XXS pack (15,360 main experts, 194.1 GiB) fits the GPUs by about 1 %: 196.3 GiB of room at 4 batch banks (+170 experts), +24 at 8 banks (the 8-bank figure rests on an inferred memory cost). Re-quantizing the drafter's experts too adds about 0.6 GiB of margin.
- Every such pack puts all experts on GPUs and moves much more work to the MI50s (about 11,800 of 15,360 experts). Whether that is faster is not a given and has not been measured. It was shelved for quality and value reasons (QA-15).

## Context: the community quants of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (desk survey, 20 Nov; nothing downloaded or run)
- **unsloth** publishes the only full 1- to 8-bit ladder with published KL: UD-Q2_K_XL is its best 2-bit (about 200 GB of backbone) and UD-IQ3_XXS its 3-bit with a real saving (about 233 GB). Its KL figures are against its own full-precision reference on its own text, so they **cannot be put on our scale** (our 2.25-bit pack: 0.044 from hosted on QA-11's method); only the ranking carries over.
- **ISTA-DASLab** (GSQ-RCO, the source of our 2.25-bit pack) also has a 2.00-bit IQ2_XXS sibling; no KL published.
- **ggml-org** has Q2_K and Q2_K_S builds; its Q2_K keeps the routed experts' down projection at MXFP4. No importance matrix.
- **No ik_llama.cpp quants** of V4.1 Flash were on the Hub, and two other well-known quantizers had published none for V4.1 Flash; ik formats would mean making our own.
- Every type these files use already has a Gary kernel, so each candidate can be scored on QA-11's harness without new code (ESTIMATE about 35–60 GPU minutes each). None has been fetched or scored.

## Verdict
- Weight space settles format efficiency at equal size (the ik formats lead) and nothing else. Which format to use for a cold tier, and whether the importance matrix helps, waits for the output-space run and a KL run.
