# DR-10: Drafting with four active users (`DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX=4`): slower on every build in a greedy, equal-length test
Status: dropped (`DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX` stays 3; one served A/B pending) · Date: 2026-11-17
Code: in Gary's release commit (no new code). Flags: `DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX` (3 in the served environment; 4 tested here).

## What and why
- With `DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX=3` a batched decode step drafts only while at most three users are active, so at four active users every step is plain (DR-04, DR-07). An earlier served test with sampling measured +11 % at four users for 4 over 3 (DR-07, REPORTED). This test asks whether letting four users draft beats plain decoding on today's builds.

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: 4 over 3 at four users × 1.05–1.45 on each build.

## Correctness
- Every run rc 0; greedy batched output equal to solo output everywhere; the MAX rule held (no drafted step above the limit); drift guard within 2.1 % (MEASURED).

## Timing
- 17 Nov, the 4 XTX + 4 MI50 test machine, the same 4 MI50 helpers as every run since 16 Nov. Four users, greedy, equal-length prompts of about 2–8K tokens. Three builds: build 17 (2.25-bit), a native MXFP4 build, and the mixed x25c pack (QA-12).

## Results
| build | MAX 3, tok/s total | MAX 4, tok/s total | 4 / 3 | label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| build 17 (2.25-bit) | 169.2 | 136.3 | **× 0.806** | MEASURED |
| native MXFP4 | 90.9 | 84.7 | **× 0.932** | MEASURED |
| x25c mixed pack | 157.2 | 123.7 | **× 0.787** | MEASURED |

- A drafted four-user step costs 2.0–2.8× a plain one (build 17: 48.0 against 23.6 ms) but lands only 1.80 tokens a round (native: 2.86) (MEASURED).
- The drafter itself is 8–16 % of a drafted step, 1.8–2.6 ms a user (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **MAX 4 is slower on all three builds; every prediction (× 1.05–1.45) is falsified.** The verify of four drafted rows costs more than the extra tokens it lands.
- One batched drafter call for all four users would save at most 6–13 % (INFERRED from the drafter's share), not enough to turn build 17 or x25c into a win; it is worth doing only once drafting already wins.
- `DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX` stays at 3 in the served environment.

## Reproduce
- Serve four users with the release build, `DS4_BATCH_DSPARK_MAX=3` and `=4`, greedy, same-length prompts; compare total tok/s.

## Notes / caveats
- **This test is greedy, with prompts of equal length that arrive together.** Served traffic samples and arrives unevenly, so steps with fewer than four active users are common. The earlier served test measured MAX 4 × 1.11 at short context and × 0.93 at 64K (REPORTED). A served A/B of MAX 3 against MAX 4 at short and long context is the open check before this is closed.
- One run a cell.
