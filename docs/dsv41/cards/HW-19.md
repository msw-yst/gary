- **Not reproduced: 1 failure in 11 runs, only in an intermediate, never in the output.** The most likely cause is the bench comparing intermediate rows the kernel does not write (stale memory), i.e. a harness artefact, not a card fault (INFERRED). A race exposed by this card's timing, or a memory fault, was not ruled out by measurement.
