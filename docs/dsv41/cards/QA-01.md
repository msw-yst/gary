Status: kept (pass) · Date: 2026-11-06

## What and why
- Check that computing experts on the MI50 does not change model quality: teacher-forced scoring of the same text with and without the helper.

## Prediction
- Gate: top-1 agreement ≥ 95 % and median KL ≤ 1e-3.
- Pre-registered: yes (gate script).
