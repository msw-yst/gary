# DR-11: Drafting by build: the native pack drafts 2.76 tokens a round, the 2.25-bit and every mixed pack about 1.8, whatever their expert share
Status: finding · Date: 2026-11-17
Code: no new code (the drafter as DR-01, run on each pack; the mixed packs' code is internal, QA-12). Measurement: greedy speculative-decoding evaluation.

## What and why
- The drafter lands more tokens a round on the native pack than on the 2.25-bit pack. Mixed packs (QA-12) put native copies of the hottest experts on top of the 2.25-bit pack. Does a more native trunk draft better, and in proportion to its native share?

## Prediction
- Pre-registered: native at least +0.6 tokens a round over the 2.25-bit pack; x25c within ±0.2 of the 2.25-bit pack. Both held.

## Correctness
- Greedy evaluation; drafted output equals undrafted output by construction (DR-04) (INFERRED).

## Timing
- 17 Nov, the test machine: 4 × RX 7900 XTX + 4 × MI50 helpers. Greedy, 12 prompts, one pass each, per build: 2.25-bit, x25c, x30, x50 (about 19, 30 and 50 % of the experts native), native.

|---|---|---|---|

| x25c, x30, x50 (mixed) | 1.81–1.84 | 27–31 % | MEASURED |

- x30 moves 2 % of the way from the 2.25-bit pack to native, x50 0 % (MEASURED).

## Verdict
- **Expert precision is not the drafting lever.** Raising the native share to 50 % does not move drafting at all.
- The difference is in what the drafter **offers**, not what is accepted: on native it offers a full 3-token draft 90 % of the time, on the others it cuts below 3 in 69–73 % of rounds. Its confidence (the 0.3 threshold of DR-01) collapses on any pack whose non-expert tensors are quantized (INFERRED). The prime suspect is the shared non-expert part: 5- and 8-bit in the 2.25-bit pack, BF16 in native (INFERRED; QA-13 found the same part matters for quality).
- Next: a mixed pack with native non-expert tensors, and a lower confidence threshold on mixed packs.

## Reproduce
- Greedy speculative-decoding evaluation of the release build's drafter on each pack, counting tokens a round and draft lengths.
